# Coaching Model

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved
**Module:** Restaurant Domain / Server Performance
**Origin:** TASK_SERVER_PERFORMANCE_001

---

## Purpose

Coaching is how Server Performance's Gap/Opportunity stage becomes an actual intervention (see [Server Performance.md](Server%20Performance.md), "The Performance Loop"). This document defines two complementary motivational levers, three sources personalized coaching combines, the coaching-effectiveness learning loop, gamification boundaries, and the underperformance/management-evidence relationship.

---

## Two complementary motivational levers

### A. Pride / Recognition

Show the Server what they do well, their improvement, their strengths, their achievements, challenges successfully completed. This is Recognition-shaped feedback grounded in the Individual Performance Profile's actual Observed/Derived evidence — never manufactured praise disconnected from evidence.

### B. Personal Economic Benefit

Whenever reasonably derivable, translate improvement opportunities into potential additional dollars of Tips for the Server:

```text
Not only:    "Your dessert attach rate could improve."
But also:    "If you raise your dessert conversion from X% to Y%, given your typical volume
              and observed tip behavior, that could produce approximately $Z more Tips per week."
```

These are **estimates when they are estimates** — RF-One never presents estimated future Tips as guaranteed income. Any dollar figure surfaced this way must draw on [Opportunity Capture.md](Opportunity%20Capture.md)'s "opportunity value missed" and [Perceived Service Quality.md](Perceived%20Service%20Quality.md)'s Tip-evidence discipline (Observed Tip vs. Estimated Cash Tip), and must be labeled as an estimate, never a fact. The goal is for the Server to understand that improving the Restaurant's sales quality can also put more money directly into their own pocket — not to promise a specific outcome.

---

## Personalized coaching — three sources

RF-One must learn not only what a Server needs to improve, but how that particular Server responds best to coaching. The coaching system combines three sources:

```text
A. Brand Playbook                 how the Restaurant wants service and selling to occur
                                    (Brand Expectation and Personal Baseline.md)

B. Best-performing internal       behavior actually associated with successful outcomes
   patterns                        within this Restaurant (Derived from the aggregate of
                                    Individual Performance Profiles — see "Invisible
                                    Benchmarking" below)

C. Individual learning history    which interventions actually work with this specific
                                    Server (Coaching effectiveness, below)
```

RF-One may eventually learn whether a Server responds better to numeric goals, recognition, competition, micro-challenges, specific behavioral suggestions, or other approved coaching styles — but this is learned from **observed response to interventions**, never from an unsupported psychological personality label (see [Server Performance.md](Server%20Performance.md), "Purpose" — RF-One does not infer unsupported psychological, health, relationship, hormonal, emotional or personal causes).

---

## Coaching effectiveness — the learning loop

```text
Intervention
  → Subsequent Performance
    → Outcome
      → Did it help?
        → Adjust future coaching
```

RF-One must be capable of learning that a particular hint does not work for a particular Server. RF-One does **not** continuously repeat advice merely because it is theoretically correct — an intervention that has not produced an observed improvement over a reasonable number of attempts is itself Evidence, feeding back into what that specific Server's Individual Performance Profile records about their responsiveness to that style of intervention (Coaching Model source C, above).

This loop is what [Service Copilot](../Service%20Copilot/README.md)'s "After Service" phase closes on every service occasion — see [Service Copilot/Service Copilot.md](../Service%20Copilot/Service%20Copilot.md).

## Gamification / comparison

Two distinct, approved uses of peer comparison:

### A. Invisible Benchmarking

RF-One may use other Servers' performance to understand achievable performance, distribution, unusual strengths/weaknesses, and internal best practices (source B, above). This does **not** require showing rankings to Employees — it is an internal reasoning input, not a default interface.

### B. Selective Gamification

Management may activate challenges, competitions, rankings, or team goals when appropriate. **Public ranking is never the default Server Performance interface** — Gamification is an opt-in, management-controlled mode, distinct from the private, individualized coaching relationship the rest of this module describes.

---

## Underperformance / management evidence

Performance should eventually provide management with an evidence history:

```text
Baseline
  → identified Gap
    → coaching/training provided
      → Server response
        → improvement or continued underperformance
```

This may help management determine whether continued employment makes business sense. **RF-One does not autonomously fire or replace Employees — final personnel decisions remain human**, and remain the exclusive authority of Personnel Management's [Personnel Decisions](../../Personnel%20Management/Personnel%20Decisions/README.md) module. The important concept this module supplies is that persistent underperformance is evaluated only after reasonable, targeted attempts to improve the person — never from a single ranking or a single observation. See [Exclusions.md](Exclusions.md).
