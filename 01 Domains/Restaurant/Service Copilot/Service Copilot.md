# Service Copilot — Three Phases

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Approved
**Module:** Restaurant Domain / Service Copilot
**Origin:** TASK_SERVER_PERFORMANCE_001

---

## Purpose

Service Copilot intervenes at three distinct moments, each with a different purpose, tone and content budget. See [README.md](README.md) for what Service Copilot consumes and its boundaries.

---

## A. Before Service — personalized briefing

A short, personalized briefing delivered before a shift/service period begins. Examples:

- one or two priorities (never an overwhelming checklist);
- recent improvement worth reinforcing;
- tonight's strategic focus (from Brand Expectation);
- a specific commercial opportunity expected tonight;
- expected personal Tip upside if that focus is executed (Server Performance/Coaching Model.md, "Personal Economic Benefit," with the same estimate-not-guarantee discipline).

**Avoid information overload.** The Before-Service briefing is deliberately minimal — one or two items, not a dashboard.

---

## B. During Service — real-time, table-specific micro-guidance

Short, actionable, context-aware guidance tied to a specific table/Dining Session. Examples:

- next product opportunity;
- strategic product suggestion;
- wine opportunity;
- dessert timing;
- an alternative recommendation;
- a reminder tied to guest/session context (e.g. a returning guest's known preference, via Dining Intelligence's Customer Consumption Profile).

During-Service guidance draws on [Opportunity Capture](../Server%20Performance/Opportunity%20Capture.md)'s progressive Available Opportunity estimate and on [Next Best Action and Next Best Moment.md](Next%20Best%20Action%20and%20Next%20Best%20Moment.md) for what to say and when. It must remain short — this is the phase [Smartwatch Interaction.md](Smartwatch%20Interaction.md) is primarily designed around.

---

## C. After Service — short feedback/learning loop

A very short feedback loop after a service occasion concludes. Examples:

- what went well;
- an opportunity missed;
- improvement versus Personal Baseline;
- progress toward Brand Expectation;
- approximate additional Tip income captured or missed (estimate, never guaranteed);
- one actionable focus for the next service.

The After-Service phase is what closes [Coaching Model.md](../Server%20Performance/Coaching%20Model.md)'s "Coaching effectiveness" loop for that specific service occasion — feeding the outcome back into the Server's Individual Performance Profile.

---

## What none of the three phases do

None of the three phases judges the Server, decides table assignment, or autonomously makes a personnel decision (see [README.md](README.md), "Boundaries"). All three remain assistive: help the Server make the best next decision, never evaluate them for its own sake in the moment.
