## 1. Formation before operational training

The dedicated [Server formation draft](SERVER_FORMATION_PHILOSOPHY_BRAND_LEGACY_001.md) develops the philosophy, Brand and legacy content and the understanding expected before pill design. It is the current working text for formation; the source map below provides supporting traceability.

**Product Owner direction, 2026-10-27:** before defining operational training, establish where the Servers work, the restaurant's philosophy and its legacy. Formation precedes training. This direction corrects an earlier draft that moved too quickly to operational capabilities.

Here, formation means understanding the reality the person joins and the reasoning they are expected to carry into their work. The Product Owner confirmed that philosophy and legacy are already expressed in the course and developed in greater detail in the supplied Legacy documents. The task is to extract, reconcile and teach that existing foundation, not ask the Product Owner to recreate it.

Legacy includes the transferable model of thought, knowledge and decision-making that enables the company to continue and evolve coherently beyond the founder's physical presence. Pino's response to principle 11 explicitly rejects reducing this to a story about the founder or obedience to past wishes. Formation must help people understand and apply that model at the level relevant to their role.

## 2. Intended Server outcome

The course provides the restaurant-specific reference for this role. Its recovered text describes a Server who delivers Rome's Flavours' Italian experience through product knowledge, professional communication, service technique, coordination and practical work. Training should connect these expectations to observable outcomes and the knowledge needed to achieve them.

This is a Rome's Flavours specialization, not a universal Server standard for every restaurant using RF-One.

## 4. Knowledge delivery and practical learning

The recovered course text describes explanations, questions during learning, product tests, practical demonstrations and shadowing. RF-One should preserve the distinction between knowledge acquisition and practical verification when mapping this material to pills.

The initial training map should relate each outcome to existing content, identify missing or outdated content, propose individual pills and state which outcomes also require observation or practice. A course section is not automatically a single pill, and existing menu pills cover only part of this course.

For an employee already hired, assess the applicable outcomes using relevant existing evidence and targeted checks. Propose training for demonstrated gaps and assessment for unverified knowledge. Selection results can supply evidence when available; they are not required to enter this path.

Use the existing pill-level Capability semantics from [Pill assessment and training readiness](../../Shared%20Domains/Selection/SELECTION_PILL_ASSESSMENT_AND_TRAINING_READINESS_001.md). Do not infer a Capability score from reading completion or invent a conversion from the existing quiz score. Trainer confirmation of a proposed path remains required by the existing specification.
