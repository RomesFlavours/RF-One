Bind the child to its parent conversation and keep its WHO fixed. Include the unsaved extractor in the child preview. Nested profiles save for future only, with no historical transaction edits; the final parent application controls existing rows and retains safe rollback. Changes invalidate older previews.
4. Tests: complete nested profile/final application/rollback; unselected-row and changed-WHO rejection; future/archive binding. Browser fixture uses the production renderer with sample data to verify Photo, passed WHO, save-return, hidden composer and delayed final choices. No real transactions changed by testing.
