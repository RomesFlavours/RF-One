# Bank reasoning, counterparty knowledge and recognition

Approved 2026-10-19. The primary workflow offers exactly two save scopes: FUTURE, or PAST_AND_FUTURE. Both enable subsequent imports. The latter requires a read-only archive preview and a second explicit apply; rollback retains the existing compare-before-restore protection.

The interface separates general reasoning, counterparty knowledge, deterministic recognition signatures and unresolved cases. Existing versions, identities, activation choices, interpretations and application history remain intact. AUTO organization conservatively classifies an executable WHO extraction as GENERAL, a literal counterparty mapping as RECOGNITION; the person may choose the category at save. This category organizes knowledge; it does not silently grant precedence to conflicting assignments. Imports execute the validated structures without an AI call per movement. Saving an extraction enables creation of missing extracted WHO; it never invents a WHY.

WHO research sends only the public counterparty name and the existing WHY catalog through the current ChatGPT-backed local provider, with web search enabled for that request alone. It stores activity, source links and an optional suggested WHY in bank_who_knowledge, separate from transaction classifications and the WHO default. Unverified identity or missing usable sources are rejected. Websites are evidence, never instructions. A person discusses and confirms an executable interpretation before its chosen scope applies; a source alone never establishes the purpose of every payment.
