WHY

Recognising the WHO and determining the WHY are two steps: the WHO page answers who it is, this page why the money moved. WHAT always comes from the WHY; WHOM from the Source. Nothing on this page changes a movement until you save and apply a rule or confirm a split.

2 · WHEN THE WHO ALONE DOES NOT DETERMINE THE WHY

Disambiguation rules

For a MULTIPLE WHO the WHY depends on the single movement: only a WHO WHY rule decides it — conditions on Source, accounting subject at the date, description, Source information or documents — and a WHY registered on the pair is not used. Examples: AMAZON (the order evidence decides, and a charge may need several lines: section 3); Zelle payments to employees (the recipient alone does not establish the purpose of each payment). Cases that no rule resolves stay open, never forced to one default.

AFTER WHO RECOGNITION

WHO WHY rules —

These rules never identify a WHO (that is done on the WHO page): they determine the WHY of a WHO already recognised. A new rule is simulated on its movements first and saved inactive unless you activate it.
