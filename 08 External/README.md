# RF-One External

## Purpose

`08 External/` holds external collaborator and source material.

---

# Authority

Material under `08 External/` is **input/reference, not RF-One architectural authority**. It becomes RF-One authority only when its content is explicitly and consciously incorporated into canonical documentation (`00 Core/`, `01 Domains/`, `02 Products/`, or `09 Strategy/`) — never silently or by mere presence in this repository.

Unlike `90 Archive/`, material here is **active/current** external reference, not historical/superseded RF-One material.

---

# What belongs here

- Documents, deliverables, and analysis produced by external collaborators (e.g. `Shelbi/`).

# What does not belong here

- RF-One's own historical/superseded documentation — that belongs in `90 Archive/`.
- Anything treated as canonical without an explicit incorporation step.
