# RF-One Meetings

## Purpose

`06 Meetings/` holds meeting notes and chronological discussion records.

---

# Authority

Meeting notes are **not canonical**. A decision discussed or made in a meeting becomes RF-One architectural authority only after it is formalized in the appropriate canonical location (`00 Core/`, `01 Domains/`, `02 Products/`, or `09 Strategy/`), typically via an approved Task under `07 Tasks/`.

---

# What belongs here

- Dated meeting notes and discussion records, in chronological order.

# What does not belong here

- Architectural conclusions presented as if already approved — record the discussion here, then formalize the decision in the relevant canonical location.
