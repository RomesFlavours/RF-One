# Approved strategic direction

The following strategic principles are approved for canonicalization under `09 Strategy/`.

## 1. RF-One is business-first

RF-One is ultimately intended to create measurable economic value through business.

The Core may remain universal and applicable to non-business Domains, but RF-One's commercial priority is B2B business value.

Do not convert this commercial priority into universal Core ontology.

---

## 2. RF-One is a Business Autopilot under human command

The strategic Product direction is not merely recommendation software.

Within Delegated Authority, RF-One should increasingly:

- observe;
- interpret;
- detect problems and opportunities;
- decide;
- execute;
- measure Outcomes;
- learn;
- correct course;
- escalate when required.

Human control does not imply continuous human operation.

Do not duplicate the Core definition; Strategy should reference it and explain its commercial significance.

---

## 3. Economic value is commercially central, not ontologically universal

The historical objective:

```text
Maximize Economic Profit
```

must **not** become a universal Core Goal.

Canonical Strategy should instead state that RF-One commercial Products should aim to create measurable economic value for business customers, subject to:

- confirmed customer Goals;
- Constraints;
- Delegated Authority;
- law/policy;
- acceptable risk;
- available Domain knowledge;
- sufficient Reality information.

Do not imply RF-One may override customer Goals in pursuit of profit.

---

## 4. Cash-Based Profit is a historical metric candidate

The historical definition of Cash-Based Profit is strategically useful but must not be declared the universal or permanent RF-One economic metric.

Treat it as:

```text
historical metric candidate / possible Product metric
```

that may be used where appropriate.

Strategy should leave room for other measures such as:

- margin;
- contribution;
- cash flow;
- EBITDA;
- revenue;
- avoided cost;
- labor productivity;
- inventory efficiency;
- risk reduction;
- capital efficiency;
- customer lifetime value;
- other Domain/Product-specific measures.

Do not create a detailed finance ontology in this task.

---

## 5. Counterfactual value measurement is strategically important

RF-One should aim to estimate the value it generated by comparing:

```text
actual observed outcome
```

against:

```text
estimated counterfactual outcome without RF-One intervention
```

where such estimation is epistemically defensible.

This is important for B2B ROI demonstration.

### Required safeguards

Do not present an estimated counterfactual as fact.

It must remain subject to the Epistemic Boundary.

Any value attribution should make uncertainty, assumptions, and evidence visible.

Do not redefine generic Core Outcome around financial counterfactuals.

---

## 6. Optimization scope

Do not preserve the legacy absolute phrase:

```text
Unlimited Optimization Scope
```

as written.

Approved strategic formulation:

RF-One may optimize across any business area for which it has:

- relevant Domain knowledge;
- sufficient Reality information;
- compatible confirmed Goals;
- Delegated Authority;
- applicable Constraints;
- acceptable risk;
- legal/policy permission.

The breadth of optimization should expand as RF-One gains valid knowledge and authority.

This is a Product/commercial scope principle, not Core ontology.

---

## 7. Operational and strategic horizons

RF-One should reason across multiple economic time horizons.

Short-term optimization must not silently destroy long-term value.

Strategy should recognize at minimum:

- operational horizon;
- strategic horizon.

Do not require a rigid two-bucket model if more granular horizons are useful later.

---

## 8. Business Knowledge Platform positioning

Preserve the strategic insight that RF-One's proprietary value lies primarily in the accumulated and structured combination of:

- Core ontology;
- Domain knowledge;
- Subject/Reality modeling;
- Decision and Outcome memory;
- temporal coherence;
- epistemic discipline;
- orchestration;
- autonomy logic;
- Process knowledge;
- constraints;
- learning;
- governance.

Commodity intelligence engines, models, infrastructure, OCR, APIs, databases, and other technologies may be bought rather than rebuilt when appropriate.

Software is essential execution machinery, but the strategic asset is broader than software code alone.

Do not claim software has no value.

---

## 9. Service / SaaS strategy

The historical statement:

> RF-One must be delivered as a service and can never be sold as software.

must **not** be canonicalized as an immutable law.

Canonical Strategy should state:

- centralized/service delivery may strongly support knowledge governance, continuous improvement, shared platform intelligence, operational control, and commercial defensibility;
- SaaS/service delivery is the current strategic preference where it best protects and compounds RF-One's knowledge advantage;
- future delivery models remain Product Owner decisions and may evolve.

Do not convert delivery strategy into Core architecture.

---

## 10. Shared intelligence / knowledge network

Preserve the strategic insight that RF-One may become more valuable as generalized knowledge learned across deployments improves the platform.

However, cross-customer learning is never automatic.

Any future shared-intelligence model must preserve:

- tenant isolation;
- confidentiality;
- privacy;
- contractual restrictions;
- data ownership;
- provenance;
- governance;
- epistemic quality;
- abstraction/anonymization where required.

Customer-specific information must not automatically become shared platform knowledge.

Only appropriately generalized, permitted, governed, and validated knowledge may be promoted beyond its originating context.

---

## 11. Knowledge governance

Strategy should distinguish between:

```text
customer-specific knowledge
Domain knowledge
Product knowledge
platform-level generalized knowledge
Core ontology
```

Promotion from one level to another requires explicit governance.

AI may identify candidate knowledge, evidence, patterns, contradictions, or possible generalizations.

AI must not silently rewrite canonical Core or shared Domain knowledge based only on one customer instance.

Do not design the full production governance workflow in this task.

That belongs later to Product/Runtime architecture.
