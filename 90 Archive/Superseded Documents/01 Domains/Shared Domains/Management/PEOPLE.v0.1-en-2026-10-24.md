# People

**Status:** Functional reference consolidation, not an approved implementation specification.  
**Sources:** P01 §§1–12.4 (PDF pp.1–9); M03 §3; C01 points 1–3; R03, R04, R06–R09.  
**Open issues:** G01–G06, G10–G15, G20.

## Purpose and evolution of scope

M03 §3 describes two sources of understanding: self-definition/experience and continuous observations from others, producing one evolving professional profile. P01 §§1, 3 expands this into four functions: **understanding, assistance, coaching/training and assessment**. Preserve both statements: the later People specification is a **functional draft / proposed consolidation**, not automatic approval to move capabilities from other domains.

P01 applies to owners, managers, employees and authorized collaborators, regardless of hierarchical level. Its proposed cross-domain relational role uses other domains' knowledge without taking their operational rules or decision authority (§10).

## Identity and context

P01 §4 calls for one People profile linked to existing RF1 account management, even across locations, companies and Business Domains, while keeping company contexts, data and permissions separate. No parallel personnel registry is created. Function access does not imply visibility of all People information. R03 Person Continuity is itself a draft requirement, not a selected technical identity solution. Cross-company linking and access mechanics remain open.

## Initial conversation

P01 §5 specifies a free-flowing AI conversation with no mandatory ordering of characteristics/questions. AI chooses topics, examples, contradiction checks and when enough information exists to conclude. Pause/resume is supported; unexplored characteristics need not be assessed. **This is separate from Organization's fixed core interview sequence.**

Coaching may occur during the interview. Record separately what was known before teaching, what was learned during the exchange and what was later applied. Newly taught knowledge is not already demonstrated capability. These are P01 draft behaviors.

## Continuous observations and confidentiality

M03 §3.1 and C01 point 2 report the 21 October compromise:

- Authorized contributors can submit observations at any time; AI may request task-specific observations.
- AI knows authors to distinguish independent from repeated reports.
- No human user, including managers, Pino or Shelbi, can inspect individual observations or discover authors.
- Share concerns and verifiable workplace facts, never the comments or identifying quotations/details.
- Unsupported concerns remain “to verify.”
- The person can respond and provide evidence; human review precedes use in decisions.

P01 §§6–6.2 explicitly extends the raw-contribution restriction to Admin and validator. AI should explore situations constructively and recognize unfounded reports, not seek confirmation alone. An ordinary configuration permission must not silently override this restriction. Enforcement, access surfaces, retained derivatives and safe summaries require practical specification (G10).

M03 classifies universal characteristic meaning as Legacy; technical content comes from the industry/role. Hospitality and “hosted, not served” belong to different layers. Four descriptive levels are **not observed, with support, independently, able to teach others**; no numeric weights, scores or thresholds are specified there. Do not turn these levels into a ranking.

Assess observable professional conduct, not diagnoses, personality labels or private life. M03 §3.3 reports that people managing/guiding others, including consultants, are assessed on **1–12 and 15–17**, excluding 13–14 and assessment of hours/schedules/methods; deliverable engagements are assessed against outputs, deadlines and commitments. The exact application of that exclusion beyond consultants needs clarification (G20).

## Living profile, time and correction

P01 §7 proposes identity/context, capabilities, evidence, development and history in the profile. Distinguish factual information, declarations, observations, interpretations and validated conclusions. Incorrect information is marked and stops supporting assessments.

P01 §7.1 explicitly reports an agreed rule that professional history is retained while the person remains with the company, subject to applicable obligations. After-departure handling is undefined. Organization's raw-interview deletion proposal is separate, not a People retention rule.

P01 §7.2 proposes relevance based on elapsed days, differentiated by evidence type/characteristic and configured in Management Configuration. It specifies **no formula or values**. M03 prohibits numeric characteristic scoring; whether/how evidence relevance differs from scoring requires resolution (G12). Reduced relevance does not change historical facts. Isolated events and newer evidence must not become permanent labels.

## Human validation

P01 §8 proposes a confidential preliminary profile for the configured validator: proposed conclusions, workplace facts, supporting and contradicting elements, uncertainty and further investigation. The validator may approve, correct, reject or request investigation, but sees no original comments/authors. P01 names **Shelbi for initial development**; this is a draft designation, not an existing authority grant.

The person can explain, correct and provide evidence. Materially changed conclusions require renewed validation; validation does not make uncertain interpretation certain. Significant people decisions remain with competent humans. M03's response-before-use compromise must remain visible when defining P01's validation/conversation sequence (G13).

## Assistance, coaching and autonomy

P01 §§9, 12 proposes both requested and AI-initiated assistance, immediate short exchanges or schedulable training, with later checks of application. Before an initiative, evaluate objective, context/missing information, benefit, consequences, less intrusive alternatives, reversibility and authority. Verification is proportionate to consequences, not AI confidence alone.

Understanding or forming a judgment is not authorization to act. Low-risk permitted initiatives may proceed; actions requiring approval await it; prohibited actions are blocked. People cannot change working conditions, responsibilities, official assessments, domain rules or its own permissions. RFCore changes remain outside this task.

## Outputs and handoffs

People contributes contextualized capability knowledge and development/support needs to Organization and Project Manager; only necessary authorized information may reach other domains. Exact recipients, fields, visibility and adequacy criteria for assignment use are open. Do not export the full confidential profile as the default interface.
