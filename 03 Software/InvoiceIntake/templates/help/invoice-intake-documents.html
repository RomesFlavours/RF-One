Documenti acquisiti

Every saved purchase document, newest first, including those accepted automatically (NORMALIZED): the original and the data read always stay consultable. The values shown already include any correction made in Human Review.

Fields and columns

#
Document number. Click it to open the document in Human Review.
Fornitore
Supplier.
Numero
Invoice number.
Data
Invoice date.
Totale fattura
Invoice total.
Saldo
Balance still due, when the invoice shows one. It is never used as the invoice total.
Righe
Number of lines.
Lettura
How the document was read (for example PDF-Text or Textract-AnalyzeExpense).
Stato
NORMALIZED: accepted automatically or after review. HUMAN: waiting for a person in Human Review.
