Purchased Human Review — coda

The documents that could not be accepted automatically (state HUMAN) and are waiting for a person, newest first. Documents accepted automatically are not listed here; they are in the list of all documents.

What you can do

  • See who you are working as and change it with cambia (it opens the reviewer identification page, where you declare name and role again).
  • Open a document with Apri to compare it with the original{% if reviewer_role is defined and reviewer_role in ("REVIEWER", "VALIDATOR") %} and confirm or correct it{% endif %}.
  • Go to Supplier + Format training to see how reliable each supplier's document format has proved, or to Mailbox for the invoices received by e-mail.

Fields and columns

Revisore
The name and role you declared. VIEWER can only look; REVIEWER can also confirm and correct; VALIDATOR can also validate a supplier's format.
Creato
When the document was saved.
Supplier
Supplier, including any correction already made in review (“Unknown Supplier” when none was recognised).
Pendenti/Supplier
How many documents of the same supplier are waiting in this queue.
Numero, Data, Totale
Invoice number, date and total, with corrections already made.
Source
The original file, with the pages of this invoice when the file contained several; “(multi-invoice)” marks a document split from such a file.

Buttons and what happens

Apri
Opens the document review page. Opening it changes nothing.

Next steps

A document leaves this queue only when a review is completed and nothing blocking remains. When the queue is empty, no document is waiting.
