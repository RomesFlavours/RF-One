Supplier + Format training

How reliable the reading of each supplier's documents has proved, separately for each format (how the document was read and whether it came directly from the supplier or through Instacart). Every completed review adds one observation automatically; nothing has to be entered by hand.

Fields and columns

Supplier
The supplier.
Format
How the document was read, followed by its channel, for example “PDF-Text/Direct” or “Textract-AnalyzeExpense/Instacart”.
Reviewed
Number of completed reviews for this pair.
Normalized / Human
How many of them ended NORMALIZED or stayed HUMAN.
Streak corretto
Consecutive reviews that ended NORMALIZED. A review that stays HUMAN resets it to zero.
Soglia
The streak needed before the format can be validated (5 unless configured otherwise).
Trust state
UNTRAINED: new pair. TRAINING: a person has started training it. VALIDATED: confirmed reliable by a person. DEGRADED: was validated, but a review stayed HUMAN or the document layout changed, so it is no longer trusted. Moving a pair to TRAINING, or back from DEGRADED, is not possible from this page.
Eleggibile
“ELIGIBLE FOR VALIDATION” when the pair is in TRAINING and its streak has reached the threshold; underneath, the reason.
