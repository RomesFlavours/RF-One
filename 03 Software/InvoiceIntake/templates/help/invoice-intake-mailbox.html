Invoices received by e-mail

This page shows the most recent invoice attachments collected from the invoice mailbox (up to 200), with what happened to each one. It is a view only: the mailbox is collected by a separate process started by hand, and this page neither fetches new mail nor changes anything.

Fields and columns

Mailbox
The mailbox being collected, or a notice that it is not configured on this computer. The number after it is how many attachments are listed.
Ricevuta
When the e-mail was received.
Mittente
Sender of the e-mail.
Allegato
Name of the attachment.
Fornitore
Supplier of the saved document, once the attachment has been saved.
Stato
PENDING: being processed. DELIVERED: passed to the same reading used for manual uploads and saved. DUPLICATE_TECHNICAL: the same file had already been delivered, nothing new created. FAILED: not delivered.
NORMALIZED/HUMAN
State of the resulting document: accepted automatically, or waiting for Human Review.
Errore/Retry
The failure reason and how many times it has been retried. Failed messages are tried again by the next mailbox collection run.
