How to use the Transactions window

1. What am I looking at?

C-Bank lists the bank movements of this run that are not fully reconciled yet. On the dashboard they are grouped by problem (for example NO_PROFILE = the WHO is known but has no Recognition Profile) and by WHO (for example MERCHANT BANKCD). Clicking a group opens only its movements here.

Previous Bank classifications are proposals recovered read-only from the old Bank (often AI batches that were never reviewed, shown as their origin). They are not approved decisions until you approve them here.

2. Understanding the columns

Paid byThe owner of the account or card that paid (e.g. a payment from Checking RFWP is paid by RFWP).
WHOThe counterparty: who was paid or who paid us (e.g. ADP WAGE PAY, TABLE TOP LINEN).
BeneficiaryThe LLC or person the movement economically belongs to. It can differ from Paid by: if RFWP pays a bill of RFMD, the beneficiary is RFMD and RFMD owes RFWP.
WHYThe nature of the movement (e.g. Payroll Wages Payment, Merchant / Card Sales Settlement).
Category RF-OneThe management category (e.g. 1210 Merchant Processor Receivable / Clearing). Usually it follows the WHY.
QBThe QuickBooks account. It shows not mapped for now: the QuickBooks chart of accounts is not imported yet.
OperationThe type of operation: operating expense or income, capital asset, financing, transfer between accounts, card payment, inter-entity advance/repayment, other patrimonial.
StatusRECONCILED = every needed field is set. EXCEPTION = something is missing or uncertain; the reason is listed below it. 🔒 = a protected human decision.
vs BankComparison with the old Bank: SAME, DIFFERENT (with the fields), CBANK_OPEN (still open in C-Bank), BANK_EMPTY (Bank had nothing).

3. How to approve transactions

  1. Look at the movements of the group in the table (click a row to see the full decision trace; use Previous / Next to move through the list).
  2. In Previous Bank classifications, check each proposal: Beneficiary, WHY and Category.
  3. If a value is wrong, change it in the proposal's drop-downs before approving (choosing a WHY also fills in its category).
  4. Select these rows ticks the movements of that proposal; you can untick the ones you are not sure about.
  5. Approve selected rows approves only the ticked movements of that proposal.
  6. Approve whole group approves every movement of that one proposal, not every movement visible in this window.

Check before approving: the old Bank may have given the same classification to different movements. Example: in MERCHANT BANKCD the proposal “RFWP · Merchant / Card Sales Settlement” holds 20 card-sales deposits, while the proposal “RFMD · Merchant / Card Sales Settlement” holds one −45.00 debit that the AI flagged as a possible processing fee: a debit is not a sales deposit, so correct its WHY (e.g. Bank Service Charge) or leave it open. Untick or correct any row you are unsure about. Movements with a different beneficiary are always in a separate proposal. A proposal marked incomplete (e.g. no category) must be completed first, otherwise the rows stay in EXCEPTION.

4. Investigate with AI

Use Investigate selected with AI when the information is really uncertain: no previous classification, conflicting rules, or a beneficiary you cannot tell. Tick up to 6 open movements that look alike; ChatGPT (OpenAI) studies them together and judges each one.

The AI only proposes. A movement is classified automatically only if the AI says the evidence is sufficient with confidence ≥ 0.85; otherwise you see its question and alternatives on the movement (for example: “Does this payment settle payroll already accrued, or cover unrecorded wages?”). You can also discuss a single movement in its own window (“Conversation with AI”).

5. What happens after approval?

  • The decision is saved only in C-Bank Prototype (its experimental store).
  • The operational Bank is not changed.
  • An approved row becomes RECONCILED if WHO, Beneficiary, WHY, Category and Operation are all set; otherwise it stays in EXCEPTION with the missing field shown. “Just approved” shows the result at once, and the dashboard counts update when you close this window.
  • No permanent rule is created, and an approval applies only to the rows you approved, never to other movements of the same WHO.
  • Approved decisions are protected 🔒: automatic processing never changes them, and approving the same row again is refused.
How to use this window
  1. Read the AI question in the yellow box.
  2. Select an answer, or choose Other and explain in your own words, then Submit answer.
  3. Review the proposed classification: the AI fills WHO, Beneficiary, WHY and Category; nothing is saved yet.
  4. Confirm the decision with Confirm human decision (saved only in C-Bank).
