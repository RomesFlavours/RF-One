How to use the Transactions window

1. What am I looking at?

C-Bank lists the bank movements of this run that are not fully reconciled yet. On the dashboard they are grouped by problem (for example NO_PROFILE = the WHO is known but has no Recognition Profile) and by WHO (for example MERCHANT BANKCD). Each group row has one button, Review: the button and a click on the row open the same group window. Its title gives the WHO and the number of movements to classify; below it, the concrete reason the movements are open (e.g. “WHO recognised, WHY to determine”). The window lists the group's movements (date, description, amount, account/card) and, when they exist, earlier classifications to approve. Top right: Define rule and Advanced details (this technical list, with AI investigation of selected movements and the decision trace).

Previous Bank classifications are proposals recovered read-only from the old Bank (often AI batches that were never reviewed, shown as their origin). They are not approved decisions until you approve them here.

2. Understanding the columns

Paid byThe owner of the account or card that paid (e.g. a payment from Checking RFWP is paid by RFWP).
WHOThe counterparty: who was paid or who paid us (e.g. ADP WAGE PAY, TABLE TOP LINEN).
DestinationThe LLC or person the movement economically belongs to. It can differ from Paid by: if RFWP pays a bill of RFMD, the beneficiary is RFMD and RFMD owes RFWP.
WHYThe nature of the movement (e.g. Payroll Wages Payment, Merchant / Card Sales Settlement).
Category RF-OneThe management category (e.g. 1210 Merchant Processor Receivable / Clearing). Usually it follows the WHY.
QBThe QuickBooks account. It shows not mapped for now: the QuickBooks chart of accounts is not imported yet.
OperationThe type of operation: operating expense or income, capital asset, financing, transfer between accounts, card payment, inter-entity advance/repayment, other patrimonial.
StatusRECONCILED = every needed field is set. EXCEPTION = something is missing or uncertain; the reason is listed below it. 🔒 = a protected human decision.
vs BankComparison with the old Bank: SAME, DIFFERENT (with the fields), CBANK_OPEN (still open in C-Bank), BANK_EMPTY (Bank had nothing).

3. How to approve transactions

  1. Look at the movements of the group in the table (click a row to see the full decision trace; use Previous / Next to move through the list).
  2. In Previous Bank classifications, check each proposal: Destination, WHY and Category.
  3. If a value is wrong, change it in the proposal's drop-downs before approving (choosing a WHY also fills in its category).
  4. Select these rows ticks the movements of that proposal; you can untick the ones you are not sure about.
  5. Approve selected rows approves only the ticked movements of that proposal.
  6. Approve whole group approves every movement of that one proposal, not every movement visible in this window.

Check before approving: the old Bank may have given the same classification to different movements. Example: in MERCHANT BANKCD the proposal “RFWP · Merchant / Card Sales Settlement” holds 20 card-sales deposits, while the proposal “RFMD · Merchant / Card Sales Settlement” holds one −45.00 debit that the AI flagged as a possible processing fee: a debit is not a sales deposit, so correct its WHY (e.g. Bank Service Charge) or leave it open. Untick or correct any row you are unsure about. Movements with a different beneficiary are always in a separate proposal. A proposal marked incomplete (e.g. no category) must be completed first, otherwise the rows stay in EXCEPTION.

4. Analyse the group and define the rule

Analyse the group and propose a rule examines every movement of the group, not a few, with the WHO's history: description formats, common nature, accounts/cards and personal or company ownership, subgroups and exceptions. The AI reads the formats in batches under the sharing already approved (at most three masked descriptions per format, never amounts or account numbers). Its state is always shown: not analysed yet, in progress, proposal available, no sufficiently supported rule, limited by missing data, technical error (retry). A proposal gives verifiable conditions, WHY and WHAT, how many and which movements it would resolve (before → after) and which stay excluded or uncertain, with the reason. A rule resting on the WHO name alone is never proposed. Nothing is saved, activated or applied. Missing files of other accounts/cards do not block it: only missing data of this group's accounts/cards are reported.

Define rule opens the WHY rule editor in this window (section 6); “Define rule with this proposal” opens it filled with the proposal’s conditions, WHY and exceptions. From a proposal or the rules panel you can also open the Bank’s recognition-profile editor, with the group context as editable text: WHAT follows the chosen WHY and an incoherent pair is refused; Preview writes nothing and must run again after a conversation change; profiles have no draft. A profile applies only to movements whose WHO is read by an extraction rule and that are still to reconcile: for a group whose WHO is already recognised the preview finds nothing to apply and the profile can only be saved for the future. Closing the editor returns to the same group; after a save the open movements are re-evaluated.

In Advanced details, Investigate selected with AI still studies up to 6 open movements; like a full run, it needs complete data.

5. What happens after approval?

  • The decision is recorded in the Bank database as a protected human decision.
  • An approved row becomes RECONCILED if WHO, WHY, Category and Operation are all set (the accounting subject is always the Source owner, decision 86); otherwise it stays in EXCEPTION with the missing field shown. “Just approved” shows the result at once, and the dashboard counts update when you close this window.
  • No permanent rule is created, and an approval applies only to the rows you approved, never to other movements of the same WHO.
  • Approved decisions are protected 🔒: automatic processing never changes them, and approving the same row again is refused.

6. Rules of every scope: single WHO, category, general

In the Review window, the panel WHO rules, category and declarations lists the WHY rules that concern the WHO: those of the WHO only, those of its category and the general ones, active or draft. New rule, Revise or Use this proposal in the rule editor open the same editor: choose the scope (this WHO only, every WHO of the confirmed or declared category, general), the WHY from the list (the WHAT derives from the WHY and is shown), verifiable conditions (e.g. personal or company ownership of the account/card, description text) and exceptions. A general rule needs at least one condition beyond ownership, direction or account type; a heterogeneous category (marketplace) cannot determine a WHY.

Simulate saves nothing. It compares the rule with every rule of the other scopes on the same movements, with no fixed precedence: equivalent (same WHY where they overlap; it can be replaced, and it is shown whether the equivalence also holds on the conditions, hence on future movements, or only on the current data), complementary (also covers other cases: kept), conflicting (a different WHY: you decide, e.g. keeping the other rule as an explicit exception). It shows conditions, rules and movements before → after, the group's movements resolved and the protected human decisions. Every change of scope, WHY, conditions, exceptions or choices cancels the simulation: run it again.

Save draft saves the rule inactive: no movement changes. Save and apply now is possible only without open conflicts: it asks for confirmation with the exact number of movements that change, repeats the simulation on the server (if data, rules or choices changed, saving is refused), activates the rule for future imports too, deactivates only the rules you chose to replace (with history and Restore, never deleted) and re-evaluates the open movements. The same save cannot be applied twice. Confirmed human decisions stay protected. After applying you return to the same group with updated counts.

7. WHO category and contextual declarations

The category describes the activity of the counterparty or service (food service, passenger transport, airline…); it is not the WHAT and not the UNIQUE/MULTIPLE mode. Propose with the AI runs an internet search with the WHO name only (no description, amount or account) and records the proposal with evidence, sources and certainty; it needs the sharing approval the Bank already requires. A proposal authorises nothing until you confirm it; you can also reject it or choose a category directly. Every proposal and decision stays in the history.

A contextual declaration is your statement about how you use a WHO, e.g. “I use UBER only for transport”. It is recorded with its text, the category it states and the Sources it covers: it gives the WHO that category only on those Sources, is not a universal property and classifies nothing by itself. Define a rule from this declaration prepares a category rule (with the Sources' ownership) to complete with the WHY, simulate and confirm. When a description or document shows a different service, exclude it as an exception: that Uber Eats always has a distinct description is not verified. When the description, the evidence and a declaration are enough, no report is needed.

8. Advanced details

Advanced details opens the technical list of the group's movements: search, selection, opening a movement, AI investigation of selected movements (up to 6; like a full run it needs complete data) and the decision trace. Closing the list returns to the Review window.

9. Movement window

  1. Read the AI question in the yellow box.
  2. Select an answer, or choose Other and explain in your own words, then Submit answer.
  3. Review the proposed classification: the AI fills WHO, Destination, WHY and Category; nothing is saved yet.
  4. Confirm the decision with Confirm human decision (recorded in Bank as a protected human decision).

Previous and Next move inside the current list; technical information is under Advanced details.
