A Selection Outcome is what operational decision currently applies to an Application (e.g. Hire, Hold, Stop, Withdrawn) — completely separate from Stage (where the Application is in the process). An Outcome may be applied at any moment, and no Outcome is ever permanently irreversible — a closed Application can always be reopened later. RF-One seeds a few common examples below; edit them freely or add your own.
