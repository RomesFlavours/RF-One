A Reminder Policy governs no-response follow-up for one trigger (e.g. "Advance to Phone — Scheduling"): how many reminders, how long to wait before each, the final response deadline, and whether RF-One may automatically apply a configured STOP Outcome (reason "NO RESPONSE") when the deadline passes with no candidate response. Automatic STOP only ever happens through this explicit, restaurant-configured opt-in.
