Configuration

The settings of this position only: its specific context and the AI features it uses. Other positions keep their own settings and do not inherit changes made here.

Fields

Position context
Service style, shifts, responsibilities, team, training and other verified facts about this opening (up to 16,000 characters). It is used when drafting the requirements and the job ad.
AI features for this position
Tick the features to use: AI position drafts, AI message drafts, Résumé-based questions, AI résumé and answer evaluation. The badge Enabled means the feature is selected and available; Disabled means it is not selected or not available. "Currently unavailable on this installation" means your choice is kept but has no effect until the installation allows it; résumé-based features need résumé processing to be authorized for the installation.
Service information
The AI provider in use, who sends the e-mails, and which company facts are known or missing. Missing facts are never invented.
Test selected AI features
The result of the last test of each feature: Passed or Failed, when it ran, the provider and, with "View test output", what the model returned.

Buttons and what happens

Save position configuration
Saves the context and the feature choices for this position and records the change in its history.
Test …
One button per enabled feature. It makes a real call to the configured AI model with a synthetic résumé and saves the result here. It sends no e-mail and does not change requirements or candidate records. Save your feature choices before testing.
