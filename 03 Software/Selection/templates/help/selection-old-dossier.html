Candidate Dossier

A two-to-three-minute read of this application's whole selection journey: screening, evidence, trainable gaps, interviews, decisions, communication, history and notes. It is never a hiring recommendation. From here you also take the application in charge and record decisions.

Sections

Header
Candidate, target role, date applied, current stage, current outcome and lifecycle; links to the original CV and the candidate page; the repeated-applicant badge, open follow-ups and any earlier training check (historical information, never an automatic rejection).
Selection Session
Shown when the application belongs to a session: the session, its role and location, any rule change that affected it, and who has the application in charge, with its ownership history.
Primary Screening
The strongest positive and negative factors, active hard disqualifiers and criteria with insufficient evidence.
Evidence Summary
Requirements with strong, weak, conflicting or unresolved evidence, and the important selection signals.
Trainable Gaps
Missing capabilities that appear trainable, with RF-One's initial level (0 to 4) and yours. Informational only: never an automatic decision or a training plan. Important gaps that are not trainable are listed apart as non-trainable concerns.
Interview Journey
The state of the phone and in-person interviews, items not yet resolved and inconsistencies to verify.
Decision & Outcome
Stage and outcome controls, the candidate communication summary (latest message, how many, any awaited response, appointments, open alerts), the application intake (source, channel, answers, questionnaire) and the outcome history.
Information / Events, Candidate History, All Notes
Factual reports, earlier applications of the person with their flags, and every note of the journey in date order.

Windows opened by the buttons

Each window has a close × and a Cancel button that closes it without saving.{% if not rfone_login_mode %} Each shows who you are acting as, with a link to change the acting identity.{% else %} Each shows that you act as your own RF-One account.{% endif %}

Take In Charge
Makes you the owner of the application. For applications of a session, only the owner (or someone with authority over the owner) can change stage, outcome, screening overrides and trainable gaps.
✎ Reassign
Gives the application to another owner. Choose the new owner and write the reason (mandatory). People without an acting identity cannot be chosen.
✎ Override (hard disqualifier)
Sets aside a hard disqualifier found by the screening, with a mandatory reason. The original detection stays visible in the history.
✎ Edit (trainable gap)
Sets your initial level of the gap (0 to 4). Choosing RF-One's level means you agree; a different level needs a reason. A note is optional.
✎ Apply Outcome
Applies an outcome at any moment, without requiring any stage to be completed. A reason is required when it changes an existing decision to a different outcome; some outcomes also require a note. The outcome may trigger the communication configured for it.
✎ Record (Information / Event)
Records a factual report (what happened, its type, who reported it, the original source). It never changes stage or outcome by itself.
✎ Add Note
Adds a note to the application; it appears in All Notes.

Other buttons

Move Stage
Moves the application to any stage, forward or backward. When moving to PHONE INTERVIEW you can also choose a communication: a link to the candidate scheduling page, a standard "you'll be contacted" message, or none.
Reopen Application
Shown for closed or suspended applications: reopens it with an active outcome and a note.
Add Scheduling Window
An exceptional time window for this application only (stage, date, start and end time, slot length, capacity). Normal windows belong on the Selection Session page.
Full Decision Summary → / Selection Audit / Explainability →
Open the full decision page or the complete audit of this application.
View full Communication History →
Every outbound and inbound message of this application.

Changes that are not allowed (for example by someone who is not the owner of a session application) are not saved: the page reloads unchanged. Messages are recorded but, in this version, no SMS or e-mail actually reaches the candidate.
