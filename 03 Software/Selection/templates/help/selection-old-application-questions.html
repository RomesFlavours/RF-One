Application Questions

Application Questions are the first-screening questions a candidate answers on the public web application form. They are different from Phone Interview questions and In-Person Interview items, which are configured on their own pages. Here you add questions, switch them on or off and check their wording with a Compliance Review.

What you can do

  • See every configured question, active or not, with its scope.
  • Add a question, limited to one Session and/or one role if needed.
  • Deactivate a question so it is no longer asked, or reactivate it.
  • Run a Compliance Review on a question and record your decision about its result.

Fields and columns

Question text
The question as the candidate will read it. Required.
Session (leave blank for any)
Ask the question only for Applications to that Selection Session; "(any Session)" asks it everywhere. The card shows "Session #…" or "Any Session".
Role (leave blank for any)
Ask it only for that role (for example SERVER); blank means "Any Role".
Response type
The kind of answer expected: Text, Yes No, Number or Scale.
Required
The candidate must answer before submitting the form.
Related Screening Criterion (optional)
Links the answer to a Primary Screening Criterion, shown on the card as "→ criterion name". Use it only where the answer genuinely informs that criterion.

Buttons and what happens

Add Question
Saves the question immediately as active. This page has no form to edit a question's text afterwards: to change it, deactivate it and add a new one.
Deactivate / Reactivate
Stops or resumes asking the question; answers already given are kept. Reactivation is refused (the page simply reloads unchanged) while the question's latest Compliance Review has an unresolved HIGH CONCERN.
Compliance Review (panel)
Shows the status of the latest review: NOT YET REVIEWED, the number of warnings, "No concerns found", or "HIGH CONCERN — blocks activation". Each warning explains the concern and may suggest a rewrite.
Run Compliance Review
Checks the current text automatically and stores a new review; earlier reviews stay in the history on the Compliance Reviews page.
Record Decision
Stores your response to the latest review: Accept Rewrite, Edit Manually, Keep Original, Deactivate or Acknowledge High Concern, with an optional final text and a reason. A reason is required for Acknowledge High Concern, and for Keep Original when the review has a HIGH CONCERN; without it nothing is saved. The decision is recorded under your current acting identity (the "Your name" field is not used). Recording a decision does not by itself change the question's text or its active status. Accept Rewrite, Edit Manually and Acknowledge High Concern lift the block on reactivation.

Next steps

Check that the questions you need are active for the Session or role being recruited, and review the history of checks on the Compliance Reviews page.
