2 · Applications

Shows how candidates can apply to this position and lists every application received, whether it came from the public application page or from a résumé uploaded by the team.

How candidates apply

Candidates start at RMAED.it, verify their e-mail with a code, choose an open position and continue to its application page. They upload a résumé and answer a short interview in English (about five minutes). They can type or, when the browser allows it, use voice input, and they can save an unfinished interview and resume it later. They never see any internal page.

Fields and columns

Application page
The public link of the position and its state: Open (accepting applications; share the link or use it as the "Apply on company site" link on Indeed), Closed (the page is still visible but does not accept new applications; reopen the position from 5 · Decisions) or Pending approval (the page opens when the position is approved). Selection does not publish to or import from Indeed.
Candidate
Click the name to open the candidate's profile. "possible duplicate" warns that the same person or document may already be present.
Latest experience, Channel, Received
The most recent role read from the résumé, how the application arrived (public application page or manager upload) and when.
Status
Application complete (résumé and interview submitted), Interview incomplete, or Résumé uploaded for a résumé added by the team, which has no online interview.
