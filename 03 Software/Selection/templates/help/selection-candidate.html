Buttons and what happens

Book appointment
Choose Type, Duration, Place / phone, the date and time and an optional note. The interview is saved in the calendar (with a warning if it overlaps another interview of the same manager) and an interview invitation is prepared in Communications. The invitation is not sent until you approve and send it.
Create time-selection link
Offer up to three future times. Selection creates a link where the candidate picks one, and prepares a "Proposed times" message containing the link, ready for approval. When the candidate picks a time, the interview is booked and a confirmation e-mail is queued automatically.
Save (Decision)
Records the chosen decision: To interview, Needs clarification, Hired, On hold or Not selected. "Not selected" requires a reason (and a note for "Other"). A new decision does not delete the previous ones; they stay in the history. No message is sent by saving a decision.
Draft message
Prepares a new message of the chosen kind (interview invitation, menu study invitation, general message, cancellation, not selected, job offer, on hold…){% if ai_policy.allows("message_drafting") %} with AI, without sharing candidate data{% else %} from the standard texts{% endif %}. Nothing is sent.
Approve and send
Saves your edits, approves the draft and queues the e-mail, sent from RomesFlavoursCareers@RMAED.it. Unavailable while the recipient address is missing or invalid. A sent e-mail cannot be recalled.
Approve without sending / Send approved email
Approves the text without sending it; you can send it later with Send approved email, or copy it with Copy text to send it another way.
Delete
Deletes a draft. Approved messages cannot be deleted.
Retry delivery
Shown when a delivery failed or its outcome is uncertain. Queues the e-mail again; for an uncertain outcome you must first confirm you checked that the earlier attempt did not arrive, to avoid sending it twice.
