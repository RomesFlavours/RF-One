4 · Interviews

The interview calendar of this position: scheduled phone and in-person interviews, times offered to candidates and still waiting for an answer, and the candidates marked "To interview" who have no interview yet. Times are in the location's time zone{% if position is defined %} ({{ position.timezone }}){% endif %}.

Fields and columns

Calendar
Interviews grouped by day: time, length, type (Phone or In person), candidate (click to open the profile) and place. "chosen by the candidate" marks a time the candidate picked from the times you offered. Cancelled interviews stay visible, crossed out.
Proposed times awaiting a response
Times offered to a candidate through a time-selection link that the candidate has not chosen yet.
To schedule
Candidates marked "To interview" without a scheduled interview.

Buttons and what happens

Schedule
Opens the candidate's profile at the Interview section, where you book an interview or offer times.
{% if _edit %}
Cancel
Shown on confirmed future interviews. Cancels the appointment at once (it cannot be restored: book a new one if needed) and prepares a cancellation message in the candidate's Communications. That message is not sent until you approve and send it there.
{% endif %}
