Selection Audit / Explainability

A complete, read-only account of how this application was handled, for review and accountability: the rules that applied, the evidence, every decision with its author and reason, communications and changes. It changes nothing.

Sections

A. Application Context, B. Acquisition
Who applied, for which role, and through which source and channel.
C. Applicable Rules
The version of the session rule set that applied, when the application belongs to a session.
D. Primary Screening & E. Evidence
Each screening run with the contribution of every criterion. The internal priority index is shown only on this audit page, never in the everyday review pages. Also the candidate's answers to the application questions and the missing-evidence questionnaires.
F. Interviews / Practical, G. Consistency, H. Trainable Gaps
Interview plans, consistency threads, and trainable gaps with RF-One's level, the person's level and the reason for any difference.
I. Decisions / Outcomes
Outcome and stage history with who and why.
J. Events / Communication
Factual events, outbound and inbound communications and scheduling, preserved as received. An event never becomes a decision by itself.
K. Rule / Ownership Changes, L. Compliance Context
Who had the application in charge over time, rule changes during the session, and compliance warnings on the criteria used.
M. Complete Notes, Repeated-Applicant History, N. Chronological Timeline
All notes, the person's earlier applications and one timeline of everything.
