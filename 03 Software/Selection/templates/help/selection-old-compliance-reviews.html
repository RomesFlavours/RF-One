Compliance Reviews

This page is the complete history of every Compliance Review run on Selection configuration (for example a Screening Criterion or an Application Question) and of the human decision recorded in response. It is read-only: past reviews and decisions are never changed or deleted.

A Compliance Review is an automatic text check: it looks for wording that refers to personal characteristics (such as age, sex, religion, nationality, disability, family status or appearance), vague subjective wording, a characteristic that cannot be judged reliably from the evidence allowed at that stage, and rules that treat missing information as a negative result. It is an aid to human judgment, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance.

Fields and columns

When
When the review was run.
Object
What was reviewed, with its version at that moment and its name or question text.
Warnings
Each concern found, with its severity: HIGH CONCERN (a reference to a protected characteristic, or missing information treated as negative) or WARNING (vague wording, or a characteristic hard to judge at that stage). "No concerns found." means the check found nothing.
Latest Decision
The most recent human decision on that review (for example Accept Rewrite, Keep Original, Acknowledge High Concern), who recorded it and the reason given.

Where reviews are run

Reviews and decisions are made on the Primary Screening Criteria and Application Questions pages, in the "Compliance Review" panel of each item. An item whose latest review has a HIGH CONCERN cannot be reactivated until someone records Accept Rewrite, Edit Manually or Acknowledge High Concern for it.

Next steps
