How Selection works

Selection manages each job opening from its first requirements to the final decision on every candidate. Candidates apply through a public application page; your team reviews them here in RF-One. Selection organizes the evidence and proposes where to look first, but it never rejects or hires anyone on its own and never produces an overall score or ranking.

How it works

  • Everything is organized by position (a job opening at a location). A position starts as a draft, becomes open when it is approved, and is closed by hand when the search ends.
  • Each position page has five steps as tabs (1 · Position, 2 · Applications, 3 · Evaluation, 4 · Interviews, 5 · Decisions) plus Configuration, where the AI features of that position are chosen.
  • Applications are compared with the position's approved requirements and placed in three proposal groups: To interview, Needs clarification and Must-have requirements not evidenced. "Not evidenced" means the information is missing, not that the requirement is unmet.
  • Messages to candidates are drafted for you, but no e-mail leaves RF-One until a person approves and sends it.
  • Dates and times are shown in the time zone of the position's location.

Typical flow

  1. {% if _edit %}Choose + New position, describe the role in your own words and enter the title, role, location and number of openings.{% else %}A manager creates the position with its title, role, location and number of openings.{% endif %}
  2. In the position's Configuration tab, add the position context and choose its AI features.
  3. In 1 · Position, write the requirements (or copy a template, or use an AI draft), the English job ad and up to six standard interview questions, then choose Approve and open position. This opens the public application page.
  4. Share the application page link. Candidates verify their e-mail, upload a résumé and answer a short English interview. You can also upload résumés yourself in 2 · Applications.
  5. Review the proposal groups in 3 · Evaluation, open each candidate to check the evidence, and compare candidates side by side.
  6. From the candidate page, book an interview or offer times to choose from; follow the calendar in 4 · Interviews.
  7. Record the decision for each candidate (Hired, On hold, Not selected…), approve and send the messages, then close the position in 5 · Decisions.

AI features, chosen per position

  • AI position drafts: proposes requirements, the number of openings when stated, and an English job ad. You choose what to use.
  • AI message drafts: prepares English messages for review, without sharing candidate data. Drafting never sends an e-mail.
  • Résumé-based questions: adds up to three interview questions about the candidate's résumé.
  • AI résumé and answer evaluation: checks the résumé and interview answers against the approved requirements; every quotation is checked word for word against the source.

A feature works only when it is enabled for the position and available on this installation. Without AI, Selection still evaluates résumés with its rule-based engines and uses standard message texts. Changes in one position never affect the others.

Who can do what

Owners and administrators manage every position. Managers manage the positions assigned to them.
