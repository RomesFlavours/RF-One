Phone Interview

This page guides the Selezionatore through the Phone Interview of one Application: a plan of questions in priority order, where answers are recorded exactly as given, plus the Escape Route for ending early and the final decision. It is never a score and never a questionnaire that must be completed in full: you can stop at any point.

Each question

Badges
Where the question comes from (CORE from the script, DYNAMIC generated from this Application's Fit Assessment or Signals, FOLLOW UP, COURTESY), importance, GATE for Sine Qua Non questions, "Mandatory within process" and status. A text explains why a generated question was included; linked requirements and Signals are shown as badges.
Record answer → Save answer
Saves the status (for example ANSWERED, PARTIALLY ANSWERED, UNRESOLVED, SKIPPED), the answer exactly as given (never rewritten) and your separate interpretation note. Once its status is anything other than NOT ASKED, the question moves from "Remaining Questions" to "Answered / History".
Gate Evaluation → Save Gate Evaluation
For gate questions: PASSED, FAILED, UNCLEAR or NOT ASKED, with an optional note. A failed gate does not stop the Application; it only suggests the Escape Route. You remain the decision-maker.
Suggest a follow-up question → Add follow-up
Adds your own follow-up question to the plan (importance defaults to the parent's).
Record this answer as Fit Assessment evidence / as Selection Signal evidence
Shown when the question has an answer and is linked to a requirement or Signal. Adds the answer as evidence (supports, contradicts or neutral; fact, derived information or inference; with a confidence) to the Application's Fit Assessment or Signal.

Buttons and what happens

Refresh plan (new evidence)
Before the interview starts, rebuilds the generated questions from the current evidence and script (questions you added are kept). After it has started, only adds missing questions; questions already asked are never removed or reordered.
Start Interview
Shown while NOT STARTED; marks the interview as in progress.
Selezionatore notes → Save notes
Saves free notes about the interview, replacing the previous notes text.
Activate Escape Route
For when a gate failed or continuing is pointless. The remaining plan is no longer presented, unanswered questions are kept as unresolved (never deleted) and up to 3 Courtesy Questions appear for a professional closing. Write the reason.
Record decision
The explicit post-interview decision: set the plan status and the Application decision (ADVANCE TO IN PERSON, HOLD or STOP) with a reason. Advance moves the Application to the In-Person stage; Hold and Stop apply the restaurant's configured Outcome, and any matching Communication Template is sent. Unresolved questions are carried forward to the In-Person Interview. For an Application in a Selection Session, only the person who has taken it in charge (or someone with authority over them), in a Session whose Rule Set is confirmed, can record it; otherwise the page reloads unchanged.

Next steps
