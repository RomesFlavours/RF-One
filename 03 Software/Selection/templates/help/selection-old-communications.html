Communication History

Every message recorded for this application: what was sent to the candidate and what the candidate said back. In this version no real SMS or e-mail provider is connected, so outbound messages are recorded with their status but do not reach the candidate, and replies arrive only when someone records them here.

Fields and columns

Outbound Communications
When, the event that triggered the message (with the reminder number for reminders), channels used (SMS, Email), language, SMS and e-mail status, whether a response is awaited or was received, "Auto-STOP (NO RESPONSE)" when the application was stopped because the candidate did not answer, and the text or subject.
Inbound Messages
When, channel, the text as received, how the system classified it (with confidence), the effective classification (and who corrected it, if anyone) and whether it opened an alert (OPEN or Acknowledged).

Buttons and what happens

Record Message
Under "Record an inbound message": choose the channel (SMS or Email) and paste what the candidate said by phone, text or e-mail. The message is saved, classified automatically and may open an alert for the team. It is kept as received.
