Acting Identity

Available only when Selection runs without the RF-One sign-in. It is not a login: it checks no password and proves nothing. It only chooses which registered name this browser acts as, so that every decision in Selection Old records who made it.

Fields

Currently acting as
The identity your actions are recorded under.
Switch to a registered identity
The list of registered identities.
Display name
The name of a new identity to register.

Buttons and what happens

Switch
From now on this browser acts as the chosen identity, and you return to the page you came from.
Register & switch to it
Adds a new identity to the list and switches to it. Registering a name does not prove you are that person.
