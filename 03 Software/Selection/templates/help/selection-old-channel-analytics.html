Channel Analytics

This page shows, for each publication of a Job Posting, how many Applications it brought and how far they progressed in the selection process. Each row is one exact Channel and placement: results are never merged across placements. The page is read-only.

Fields and columns

Channel / Placement
Where the posting was published and the specific placement (for example a city or a campaign).
Applications
Applications received through this publication.
Passed Screening
How many of them passed Primary Screening.
Ready for Phone Review / Advanced to Phone / In-Person / Hirable
How many reached each later step of the process.
Cost/App
Recorded cost divided by the number of Applications. A "-" means no cost was recorded: RF-One never estimates a missing cost.
Recommendation
An advisory suggestion with its reason: TEST when there are fewer than 5 Applications (too little data to judge), INCREASE when the share of Hirable or phone-ready candidates is strong, REDUCE when almost nobody reached phone review, otherwise MAINTAIN.

What happens

Nothing on this page changes a budget, a publication or an Application. The recommendations are suggestions only: any change to where or how you publish is decided and done by a person.

Next steps

Use the figures to decide where to keep publishing. Publications are managed from each Job Posting's page, reached from its Selection Session.
