Compare candidates

Shows the candidates you ticked in 3 · Evaluation side by side (two to six), using the same data as their profiles. It adds no score and no ranking: the comparison helps you read, the decision stays yours.

Rows

Candidate names (column headings)
Click a name to open that candidate's profile.
Proposal
The proposal group of each candidate and whether it came from AI or from the rule-based engines, plus your decision if one is recorded.
One row per approved requirement
The requirement, its type (for example Must-have) and its status for each candidate in the current proposal: evidenced, partially evidenced, not evidenced, conflicting, or to verify at interview. "Not evidenced" means the information is missing.
Recent experience
The latest roles read from the résumé and, where available, the months of direct experience.
Interview questions
One row per question of the online interview with each candidate's answer ("—" when there is none).
