Priority Policies

A Review Priority Policy decides how much each detected Selection Signal affects an Application's Review Priority (High Priority, Interesting, Standard or Low Priority). It is kept separate from the Signal Definitions themselves, which only say what is detected (configured on the Signals page). Two restaurants can use the same Signal and weigh it differently, or not at all.

How it works

  • RF-One uses the restaurant's active policy; if more than one is active, the most recently changed one is used.
  • With no active policy, or no rule that applies, every Application's Review Priority is STANDARD.
  • A Selezionatore can still confirm or override an Application's priority on the Application itself; an override is never recalculated away.

Fields and columns

Client
The restaurant these policies belong to.
Name
The policy name; click it (or the row, or Manage) to open the policy and its rules.
Status
ACTIVE or INACTIVE.
Rules
How many rules the policy contains.
Updated
When the policy was last changed.

Buttons and what happens

Manage
Opens the policy page, where you add, change, deactivate or remove its rules and rename or deactivate the policy.
Create policy
Creates a new policy with the name you typed and opens it. A new policy starts ACTIVE and, being the most recently changed, becomes the one RF-One uses: until you add rules to it, newly calculated priorities are STANDARD. Deactivate it (on its page) if you only want to prepare it. If the name is empty, nothing is created.

Next steps
