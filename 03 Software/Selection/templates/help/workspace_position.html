In Applications, “Import résumés from Indeed” is available when the browser service is configured. Paste the candidate-list URL for one job and prepare the import. Refresh progress, open the remote browser and personally complete sign-in and SMS verification. Return here and select “Start / resume downloads”. Available résumés are archived and linked to this position; existing duplicates are reused. Opening profiles may mark applications as reviewed on Indeed.

Refresh progress shows imported, already present, no résumé and retry counts. Stop closes the browser after the current operation. After an interruption, resume and sign in again; successful imports remain saved. The browser expires after 30 minutes and supports one active request at a time. Notes are not classified and no candidate is automatically excluded.
