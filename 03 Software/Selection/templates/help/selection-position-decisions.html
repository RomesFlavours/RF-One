5 · Decisions

Summarizes the final outcomes of this position, lets you close or reopen it, and keeps the complete history of what was done and by whom.

Fields and columns

Hired / On hold / Not selected
The candidates with each outcome. "Hired" also shows how many openings the position has. Outcomes are recorded in each candidate's profile, not here.
History
Every event of the position (creation, approvals, uploads, AI drafts, interviews, messages, decisions, closing) with when and who. Times use the location's time zone.
{% if _edit %}

Buttons and what happens

Close position
Asks for confirmation, then closes the position: its application page stops accepting new applications. Existing applications, decisions and history remain available. Positions are never closed automatically, not even when all openings are filled.
Reopen
Opens a closed position again with its approved content, so the application page accepts applications again.
{% endif %}

Recording an outcome does not create an employee and does not transfer the person to other RF-One areas.
