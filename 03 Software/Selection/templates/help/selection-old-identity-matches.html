Identity Matches

When a new application seems to come from a person who already exists (similar name, but no exact e-mail or phone match), the system suggests a match here. Nothing is ever merged automatically: you decide.

Fields

Pending matches
How many suggestions are waiting for a decision.
Suggestion
The new application's person, the possibly identical existing person and the confidence (STRONG or POSSIBLE), the basis of the match, a link to the new application and the existing person's contact details.
Note (optional)
Why you confirm or reject; it is kept with the decision.

Buttons and what happens

Confirm — same person
Moves the new application to the existing person, so the applications appear together in that person's history. The application's résumé, assessments, signals and notes are not changed.
Reject — keep separate
Keeps the two people separate. When in doubt, reject: keeping two records apart is always preferred to merging the wrong people.

Each decision removes the suggestion from this list. A wrong confirmation can be corrected on the application page with the manual reassignment.
