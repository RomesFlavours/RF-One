3 · Evaluation

Compares every application with the position's approved requirements and proposes where to look first. It is a proposal only: nobody is excluded, nothing is ranked and no overall score is calculated. Evaluation starts once the position is approved.

How the proposal is made

  • Selection's rule-based engines match the résumé against the approved requirements.{% if ai_policy.allows("cv_evaluation") %} AI evaluation is also active for this position: it reads the résumé and the interview answers, and each quotation it uses is checked word for word against the source.{% endif %}
  • The Must-have requirements decide the group. "Not evidenced" means the information is missing, not that the requirement is unmet.
  • Full experience indicators are available for Server and FOH Team Leader; other roles are assessed against the requirements only.

Fields and columns

To interview / Needs clarification / Must-have requirements not evidenced
The three proposal groups, with how many candidates each contains.
Candidate card
Name (click to open the profile), the source of the proposal (AI or Rules), latest experience and months of direct experience, the main reason for the proposal, "Interview incomplete" when the online interview was not finished, the next appointment and any duplicate warning.
Awaiting evaluation
Applications that have no proposal yet.
Already reviewed by the manager
Candidates you already marked "To interview" or "Needs clarification"; they leave the proposal groups.

Buttons and what happens

Compare selected candidates
