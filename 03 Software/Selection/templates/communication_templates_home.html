A Communication Template is what gets sent by SMS/Email when a Selection Stage or Outcome fires it — the Selezionatore never rewrites the standard message per candidate. Editing a Template bumps its version; every ALREADY-SENT communication keeps the exact text it was sent with, forever. Outcome-triggered Templates ("Primary Screening Stop", "Phone Hold/Stop", "In-Person Hold/Stop/Hirable"...) must name BOTH the Outcome they answer and the Stage the Application is at when that Outcome is applied; stage-advance Templates ("Advance to Phone — Scheduling/Contact", "Advance to In-Person") and Reminders are matched by trigger + stage alone.
