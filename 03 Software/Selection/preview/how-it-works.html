How Selection works

One Job, five steps, one process. Candidates apply through RMAED.it; your team manages the process here in RF-One. The five tabs are the steps: Selection, Applications, Evaluation, Interviews and Decisions. The last four always refer to the current Job, shown under the tabs.

1. Selection: create, configure and choose a Job

  1. The Selection tab lists every Job, newest first, with its Role, location, people required, creation date, status (Draft, Active or Closed) and number of candidates.
  2. Choose New Job, pick the Role and location, and continue. The Role's standard requirements and evaluation rules load into the Job automatically.
  3. In the Job form, switch requirements or rules off, change criticality and weights, or add new ones: only this Job changes, never the Role standard. Complete the English job ad and up to six standard questions, and choose the AI features for this Job.
  4. Save Draft keeps everything and returns to the list; Complete & Publish reopens it later. Confirm & Publish approves the requirements, opens the application page and makes the Job Active.
  5. Choose Process on an Active Job: it becomes the current Job of the other tabs. A Closed Job shows View history.

2. Applications: CVs, analysis and ranking

Candidates start at RMAED.it, verify their email with a code, choose an open Job and continue to Selection on AWS. They upload a résumé and answer a short interview in English; a completed application is analyzed automatically.

Use Upload CVs to add several PDF, DOCX or TXT files at once, as many times as needed. Exact duplicates are ignored, possible duplicates are flagged, and each upload is summarized. In the ranking, NEW and the Last Upload filter show what the last upload added; in the CV list, NEW marks every CV you added while the page stayed open in that browser tab, across successive uploads.

Delete, at the right end of each row of the CV list, removes a CV uploaded by mistake, after you confirm it. It is possible only while nothing has been built on that CV: not yet queued for or read by the AI analysis, and without decisions, interviews, messages or later history. A CV sent by the candidate from the application page cannot be deleted. When Delete is not possible the button is dimmed and tells you why; use Reject instead. Deleting removes the CV from the Job together with its file and the records its upload created; a document, application or person also used elsewhere is kept. The Job's history records who deleted which file and when.

For Server and Line Cook, CV Selection SP uses OpenAI GPT-6.1 Terra to read each CV once and ranks candidates with reasons, evidence, red flags and missing information. Change the order with ▲▼. Reject removes a candidate from the ranking with a reason; the CV and the analysis are kept and Restore brings the candidate back.

SP reuses the same reading for the candidate’s name and evaluation; phone numbers and email addresses are extracted locally. Existing analyses are reused until the requirements or verification rules change. A scanned PDF needs a separate transcription step, limited to the first four pages; temporary failures have at most one retry. Exact repeated scans reuse the stored text. Check the original CV if pages are missing or the reading is partial.

AI processing follows the Job’s permissions and the installation’s reviewed data settings. Haiku on AWS Bedrock remains an installation fallback when configured. You can request a new reading explicitly; it does not repeat the SP evaluation.

Nobody is excluded automatically. The ranking is a preselection priority, not a probability of success.

3. Evaluation: one candidate, the whole dossier

Click a name in Applications, or open Evaluation and search the current Job's candidates. The dossier shows identity, original CV, the analysis with requirements and evidence, work history, demonstrated and declared competences, red flags, missing information, status, decisions and the complete history of the candidate.

Missing information is not proof that a requirement is unmet. Quotations are checked against the CV.

4. Interviews and communications

From the dossier, book a phone or in-person interview, or propose times with a link for the candidate to choose. Interviews shows scheduled interviews and proposed times, in the location's time zone.

Communications drafts interview invitations, updates, cancellations and outcome messages. Review, then choose Approve and send: RomesFlavoursCareers@RMAED.it sends the email.

5. Decisions and closing the Job

Record a decision in the dossier: To interview, Needs clarification, Hired, On hold or Not selected (a rejection from the ranking is recorded as Not selected, with its reason). Decisions summarizes outcomes and keeps the Job's history.

Close the Job when the search ends: its application page stops accepting applications; existing ones remain. A Closed Job can be reopened.

Recording an outcome does not create an employee or transfer the person into Management, Training or Organization.

AI features, chosen per Job

  • AI position drafts: proposes requirements, people required and an English job ad from the Job context. You choose what to use.
  • AI message drafts: prepares English communications for review. Drafting does not send an email.
  • Résumé-based questions: adds up to three questions about the candidate's résumé to the online interview.
  • AI résumé and answer evaluation: needed for the CV analysis; quotations are verified against the source.

Choose them in the Job form (“AI for this Job”). Other Jobs do not inherit your choices.

Who can manage a Job?

Owners and administrators manage all Jobs. Managers manage the Jobs assigned to them. Supervisors can review Jobs in read-only mode.
