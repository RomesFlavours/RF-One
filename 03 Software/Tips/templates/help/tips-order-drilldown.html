Order — why this amount?

This page explains how the tips of one order were divided, recalculated for the same period you were looking at in Calculate Tips. It is read-only and saves nothing.

Fields and columns

Settlement Time
When the order was settled, in the restaurant's local time.
Voluntary Tip, Automatic Gratuity, Gross Earned Tips
The tip left by the guest, the gratuity added automatically, and their total, which belongs first to the order's employee.
Order Employee (Gross Tip owner)
The employee who owns the order (the Service Owner), with the order's state and payment state.
Refunds
If the order has refunds, a red message says so: refunds are not reflected automatically in the calculation.
Applied Rules
One row for each Distribution Rule that applied to the order: rule version, calculation base, rate, base amount, pool generated, recipient, amount allocated and why the recipient was eligible. no eligible recipient means nobody qualified, no pool was generated and the Service Owner keeps 100%. If no row appears, no rule applied to this order.
