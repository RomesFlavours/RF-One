Calculation Run (Saved Period report)

This page is the report of one Saved Period. Every figure is read from what was saved when the period was consolidated: nothing is recalculated when you open it, so the report stays the same even if a rule, a shift or an order changes later. This page is read-only.

What you find here

Header
The run number and its state: FINAL, or the current state marked not payable. Then the business dates covered, the time the operating day starts, the time zone, the rule versions applied and when the run was calculated.
The control
Voluntary Tips, Gratuity and their total; the entitlements of Service Owners and of other recipients and their total; and the Control Difference between the two totals, which must be zero. A period that does not add up cannot become final, and the difference is never adjusted to hide it. The note below says how much reached recipients other than the order's own Service Owner.
What each employee is owed
One row per employee: result (Service Owner, Host or both), voluntary tips, gratuity, gross, what was distributed away or received, and the final entitlement. The total at the bottom is added up from the rows; if it differs from the saved total, a warning says not to pay from this report until it is explained.
Validation and finalization
The restaurant's Validation Mode and what it means. For a final period: when it became final and who validated it, or that it became final automatically. For a period not yet final: what still prevents it from becoming final, if anything.

Links and what happens

Open in RF-One Web for validation/finalization
Shown for a period that is not final. It opens this same period in RF-One, where you sign in with your RF-One account. Validating there records you as the person who approved these figures and makes the period final and unchangeable. If RF-One's address is not configured, a message says so instead.

Next steps

A final period cannot be changed. To correct it, calculate and consolidate the same dates again in Calculate Tips: a new period is created and this one stays exactly as approved. Until a period is final it is not a payroll source.
