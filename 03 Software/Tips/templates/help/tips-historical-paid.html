Historical paid tips

This page shows tips recorded as already paid in an imported payment document (a PDF of past payments). It is evidence of what was paid: it does not create payments, does not create or finalize Saved Periods, and does not change bank data or entitlements. It is read-only.

What you can do

  • Choose an imported document and read the tips it records as paid, in total and per person.
  • Open a person to see each payment row with its memo, earnings period, treatment and position in the PDF.

Fields and columns

Source document
The imported document, with its file name, import number and a short fingerprint of the file. Open document shows it.
Period stated in memos
The period the document's memos refer to, in US dollars.
Recorded paid tips
Only completed payments explicitly labelled Tip or Tips, with an earnings period fully inside the stated period. Payments labelled as pay or with a blank memo are not treated as tips. Tips crossing the period boundary and tips with an unclear period are shown separately. Possible exact repeated entries are kept in the total and flagged, because the PDF has no transaction identifiers to confirm a duplicate.
Recipient / Employee link
The name as written in the document and whether it matched an employee by exact name. Needs identity review means no unique employee could be found; the payment is kept without inventing an employee.
Status / treatment
For each row: the payment status and how it was treated (included paid tip, cross-boundary, outside period, tip with period to clarify, not classified as tip, not completed and excluded).
