Tips Payment Control — Mercury Sandbox pilot

This pilot page groups the tips still unpaid into a payment cycle and, once approved, sends one payment per employee through the payment connector configured for the restaurant. It works only with the Mercury test (sandbox) environment: no real money moves. No bank or account details are shown here.

What you can do

  • See at a glance whether payment is ready, and why not.
  • Open a payment cycle with everything still unpaid.
  • Approve and pay the open cycle, acting as a person authorized to do so.
  • Link an employee to their Mercury sandbox recipient, and retry a single payment that needs attention.

Fields and columns

Summary tiles
The restaurant; the open Payment Cycle with its status and dates (or none open); Payees / Total due; the latest business date and whether it was already calculated; the connector's available balance; and whether Clover data is reconciled (READY / NOT READY with the reason).
READY FOR PAYMENT / NOT READY
The single signal saying whether Approve & Pay can go ahead, with the reason. A red message appears when a critical attention item for Tips must be resolved first.
Payment Instructions
One row per employee in the cycle: amount, whether a Mercury recipient is linked, status, the provider's status, priority and reason of a failure, the related attention item, and Detail, which expands the business dates included with gross tips, amounts transferred out and in, and the final payable.

Buttons and what happens

Start Payment Cycle Now
Opens a new payment cycle that gathers every unpaid tip entitlement since the previous cycle. Nothing is paid yet: review the instructions below first. The button is disabled when there is nothing ready to gather.
Acting as… / Approve & Pay
Shown while a cycle is open. Choose who is approving (only people authorized to approve and pay for this restaurant are listed), then press Approve & Pay. Authorization and readiness are checked again before anything is sent; if payment is not ready, nothing happens and a message explains why. When it goes ahead, the payments are submitted to the connector and the page reports how many were submitted and how many need attention. A submitted payment cannot be withdrawn from this page.
Link
Shown for an employee without a linked recipient. Type the name of an existing Mercury sandbox recipient exactly as it is in Mercury: RF-One links the employee to it (it never creates a recipient). A previous link is deactivated, not deleted.
Retry this instruction only
Shown for a payment marked NEEDS_ATTENTION. It sends again only that payment, without duplicating it; the other payments are not touched. A payment without a linked recipient must be linked first.

Next steps

The payment schedule and the connector used are set in Configuration (Payment Schedule). If the page says the payment connector is not available, a connector must be configured there first.
