Saved Periods

This page lists every period consolidated from Calculate Tips, newest first, final or not. A Saved Period keeps the figures exactly as they were when it was saved, together with the business dates, the operating-day settings and the rule versions used. Only a FINAL period can be used for payroll.

What you can do

  • See every saved period and whether it is final.
  • Open a period's report to read its figures.
  • Go to RF-One to review and finalize a period. This page itself never changes a period.
  • Open the historical paid tips imported from documents.

Fields and columns

Validation Mode
MANUAL: a period becomes final only when a person validates it. AUTOMATIC: a period becomes final by itself as soon as it adds up. It is set in Configuration.
Run
The period's number; click it to open its report.
Business Dates
The business days the period covers. Older periods saved before business dates were recorded show their start and end time instead.
Total Tips + Gratuity / Total Employee Entitlements / Control
What was collected, what is owed to employees, and whether the two match exactly. A difference is shown in amber; such a period cannot become final.
State
FINAL, or the current state of a period still waiting for validation.
Approved by
The person who validated a final period, or automatically, no validator when it became final under AUTOMATIC mode.
Calculated
When the period was saved, in local time.

Next steps

Once a period is final it can be used by payroll. To correct a period, calculate and consolidate it again in Calculate Tips: a new saved period is created and the old one stays as it was.
