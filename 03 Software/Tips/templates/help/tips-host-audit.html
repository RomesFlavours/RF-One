Explain / Audit Host Tips

This page explains, line by line, how one Host's tips were reached for a period: which orders and payments generated a share for that Host, and how much. It explains the same calculation used in Calculate Tips; it does not change it. It saves nothing.

What you can do

  • Choose a Host and a period and see the Host's total, shift by shift.
  • Compare every line with your Clover export using the order and payment numbers.
  • Open any order to see the full explanation of its tips.
  • Download the same lines as a CSV file.

Fields and columns

Host Employee
The employee to explain. The list shows the restaurant's employees, with their location; inactive employees are marked.
Start date & time / End date & time
The period, in the restaurant's local time. When you arrive from Calculate Tips they are already filled with the period you calculated.
Period Summary
The Host's total for the period. Reconciliation OK means the lines shown add up exactly to the total calculated for that Host; otherwise a warning says they do not, and nothing is hidden.
Shift
For each shift: business date, role, rule versions used and the Host's total for that shift. Each line shows the payment time, the Server, the order and payment numbers, the original tip, the Host pool generated, how many Hosts were eligible (with each one's share when more than one) and this Host's allocation.
Unresolved Items
Orders of the period whose tips could not be given to any recipient. They are shown for you to verify and are never counted as this Host's tips.

Buttons and what happens

Explain
Builds the explanation for the chosen Host and period. Read-only.
Export CSV
Downloads a CSV file with exactly the lines shown, times in local time. Nothing is saved in RF-One.
