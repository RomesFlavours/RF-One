The unattended kitchen monitor devotes its entire screen to five ticket columns, without a general header, pages or scrolling. The small top-right X exits the kitchen display without releasing or archiving orders. Clover categories exclude drinks, wine, beer and other beverages; dish names are never used to exclude food. Beverage-only orders do not occupy columns. The five columns share the full available width and are recalculated on resize. Display settings controls font, size, columns, rows and layout. Defaults are 20 pixels, five columns and one row. Oldest orders appear on the left. Long orders continue immediately to the right with a repeated title and continuation marker. A displayed order must fit completely in the available columns; subsequent orders stay queued and enter automatically when the tablet reports a release. If the oldest order alone needs more than five columns, the font is reduced to fit it. Items are grouped by Clover When Serve modifiers: First, Second and Third, with other original values kept separate. Items without this modifier are not assigned an invented timing. Within each timing group guests appear in ascending order: Guest 1, Guest 2, Guest 3. Notes, modifiers and guests remain visible. Updates are automatic. A small warning appears only for connection problems. By explicit Product Owner instruction this display exposes only the exit X; release and configuration belong to other devices.
