## Appetizer

### Caprese of Buffalo Mozzarella

**In one line:** Fresh buffalo mozzarella, ripe tomatoes and basil on a bed of arugula.

**Taste:** Fresh, Creamy, Delicate

**Description:** Imported Italian buffalo mozzarella is the centre of this simple appetizer. Its soft, milky texture contrasts with juicy tomatoes and peppery arugula, while basil and extra virgin olive oil bring a fragrant finish. Sell the quality of the ingredients and the balance of textures.

**Ingredients:** Buffalo mozzarella; Fresh tomatoes; Fresh basil; Arugula; Extra virgin olive oil

**How to present it:** Suggest it to guests asking for a fresh starter before pasta or a richer main course. Lead with the imported buffalo mozzarella. Ask whether they enjoy a simple tomato-and-cheese combination.

**Phrases:** "If you'd like a fresh start, our Caprese pairs creamy Italian buffalo mozzarella with tomatoes and basil." / "The mozzarella is the star here: soft and delicate, with peppery arugula and extra virgin olive oil."

**Wines:** Pinot Grigio , Tenuta Morer (A dry, smooth white with citrus and green-apple notes that complements the fresh tomatoes and delicate mozzarella.) / Vermentino di Gallura , Costanera (A fresh, fruity white that pairs well with the basil and arugula while balancing the creamy mozzarella.)

**Allergen notes (to confirm with the kitchen):** Milk , buffalo mozzarella

**Kitchen checks:** Confirm the mozzarella label, any glaze or dressing, and preparation arrangements with the kitchen before answering an allergy request. No bread is listed for this appetizer; do not confuse it with Caprese bruschetta.

**Dietary restrictions:** Contains dairy; not vegan. For vegetarian requests, check the cheese's rennet. No meat or cooking wine is listed, but halal or kosher suitability has not been established: check ingredients, certification and preparation requirements rather than offering a guarantee.
