General Rules

A General Rule finds the WHO inside a description format — the text between two markers. Saving it applies to future imports; Apply to Existing Transactions processes what is already imported. A WHO is assigned only when the extracted name is already a known WHO; any other name stays a suggested WHO and the transaction stays To Reconcile. General Rules never choose a WHY.
