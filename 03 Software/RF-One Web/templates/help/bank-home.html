How Bank works

Bank Reconciliation is where the business imports the original monthly downloads of its bank accounts and cards and explains every movement: WHO was paid or paid us, WHY (the business purpose) and, when known, WHAT (the accounting category), plus For Whom (the company or person that benefited). When a month is fully explained, a report per owner can be downloaded for the accountant.

How it works

  • The work is done one month at a time. A file belongs to the months of the dates inside it, never to its name or upload day.
  • Every file is linked to a Source (a bank account, card, PayPal account or purchase platform), maintained on the Source tab.
  • Nothing becomes a bank movement until you confirm the import. A file already imported byte for byte adds nothing new.
  • A WHO is given by a person on Review Transactions, or automatically by an AI rule (AI Rules tab). A WHO never decides the WHY by itself.
  • The WHO, WHY and WHAT lists and the entities are kept on the Configuration tab; the Instructions tab explains the rules in more depth.

Typical flow

  1. On this page choose the month (it opens on the previous calendar month).
  2. 1 Import: select the month's CSV downloads and press Upload and review. An import session opens where you set the Source of each file, read and check the rows, decide possible duplicates and press Confirm Import.
  3. 2 Check Sources: open the month for source control, explain every expected account or card that has no file, then complete the month.
  4. 3 Automatic WHO and 4 Review Missing: open Review Transactions for the month, give each transaction its WHO and WHY, then on the Reconciled tab set For Whom and Confirm.
  5. On Monthly Export choose the month and the owner, check that nothing blocks the report and download it.

This page: Import and Review

Month bar
‹ and › move to the previous or next month; the month list, the year and Go jump to any month. Next to the month name you see its control state (Controlled, Historical, Provisional or Not under completeness control) and whether it is opened, incomplete or complete.
1 Import
How many source files cover the month and how many need attention. Upload and review sends the selected files with the Source left on automatic (choose a Source only if RF-One cannot recognise the file); it saves nothing yet and opens the import session. View source files opens the list of the month's files, with files of other months that still wait for an action. Each file shows its format, Source, rows, period and status; its action button (for example Resolve instrument, Normalize, Review issues, Reprocess or View transactions) does the next step for that file. Change Source moves an imported file and its transactions to another Source; a reason is required, the change is recorded in the Source assignment history and can be changed again later. Reuse for this source saves the choice as a file recognition rule for the next import.
2 Check Sources
Expected, Received, Missing / unresolved and Needs confirmation for the month. Open month starts the source control for a month not opened yet. Resolve issues or Source details opens the account-by-account table: for an account or card with no file, Resolve missing source records a decision (No activity, Closed, Lost, Replaced, Other, Source file missing, Not expected; Still active for an inactive Source) and Clear resolution withdraws it. Closed, Lost, Replaced and Other end the Source's life and are dated from its last posting. Complete Month is possible only when every expected account or card is covered by a file or resolved; Reopen Month needs a reason. Completeness control settings holds Set control start (the first controlled month, always the first day of a month) and Set validated through (months after it stay provisional: shown, not enforced).
3 Automatic WHO
How many of the month's transactions have a WHO chosen by a person, a WHO from another automatic decision (an AI rule), or no WHO yet. Bulk authorization of automatic WHO is not available. Review WHO opens Review Transactions on this month.
4 Review Missing
How many of the month's transactions still need a person, by the same definition the Monthly Export uses to block a report. Review transactions opens Review Transactions on this month.
