Your RF-One workspace

MyRFOne shows the operational modules assigned to your account. Select a module to open it using your existing RF-One session.

Kitchen opens the shared recipe workspace: Dashboard, Recipes and Preparations, and Configuration. Recipe composition and standard plating are managed from each recipe. Available sections follow your account access.

Administration contains the administration tools available to you. Settings contains your profile and, for administrators, account and organization settings. A module that is not assigned to your account is not shown; adding a Home entry does not grant access.
