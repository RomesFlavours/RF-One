Person's page: resend the invitation

This page shows one person, identified by the Registry ID of their card, with their work sites (if any) and the state of their Training invitation. Selection, the Server Course and Invite student create the first invitation; a person never has two active invitations, even with different addresses. To send it again, open this page from Students → Invitations waiting for activation (or from the link shown when an invitation already exists).

Resend invitation sends the same invitation again: the same card and the same training path. Keep the recipient to resend it to the registered address, or correct the address to send the same invitation to the new one. The email is sent first: if the email service does not accept it, nothing changes — the link already sent and the registered address still work. When it is accepted, the new link is the one that works (and the corrected address becomes the recipient). At most one resend per minute and five per hour. An address that belongs to another RF-One identity is refused.

Once the person has activated the invitation they sign in with their username and password; the page says so and nothing can be resent. Without an active invitation the page links to Invite student.
