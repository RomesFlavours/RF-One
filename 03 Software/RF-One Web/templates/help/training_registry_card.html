Resolve card and site

This page is for a Trainee who is in the Registry as a person but has no Registry card yet (for example a trainee enrolled before cards were created by Training). Confirm hire stays closed until the card exists, because the hire is recorded on that card.

Choose the Training site and role (only sites you may work on) and select Save card and site. RF-One first looks for an existing card with the same email or phone. If exactly one card has the same email, it is reused. If the match is uncertain (same phone only, several cards, or a different phone) the page asks whether it is the same person: choose the card, or «A different person» to create a new card. Nothing is created or linked until you choose. A card that already belongs to another person is never reused: ask for a Registry review.

Saving links the card to the trainee and assigns the training site; the status stays Trainee. No invitation is sent and nothing is created in Clover. Back on Students, the hire can then be confirmed as usual.
