Domain Access

This page decides which Domains one account may use, and with which role. The name under the title is the account you are changing. Only RF-One administrators can open it. What you save here changes immediately what the person sees on their Home and what they can open, also by direct link.

How access works

  • Each Domain appears in its own box. Choose No access, Whole Domain or, for Domains divided into sections, Only selected items.
  • Whole Domain includes every section of the Domain, also the sections added to it in the future. Only selected items gives only the sections ticked below the Domain; sections added later are not included.
  • Ticking a section automatically selects Only selected items for that Domain. Choosing Only selected items without ticking any section is refused.
  • A section hidden from a person is closed to them also by direct link, not only removed from the menu.
  • Some Domains offer a single checkbox instead of the three choices: ticked means access to the whole Domain, unticked means no access.

Fields

Role
User uses the Domain within its normal limits. Domain administrator may change every content of that Domain, including what other people wrote, without becoming an RF-One administrator. The role has effect only together with access to the Domain. For Training the roles are No Training role, Student, Trainer and Domain administrator (Trainer rights); if the account is already linked to a Training identity, its Training role is updated together with the access. A role shown as "(not recognised)" is an older value kept as it was; choose a listed role to replace it.
Section checkboxes
The sections of the Domain, as they appear in its own navigation. They count only when Only selected items is chosen.

Buttons and what happens

Save
Saves the choices for every Domain on the page together and returns to the Accounts list. If one choice is not valid, nothing at all is saved and the page shows the reason. You can come back and change the access at any time; removing access does not delete anything the person wrote in the Domain.

Next steps

The person sees the Domains you granted the next time their Home is loaded. Remember that an RF-One administrator also needs Domain access here to enter a Domain.
