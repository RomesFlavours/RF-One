Attention Items

An Attention Item is a request for a person's attention raised by a business Process. This page lists the Attention Items on file, newest first, with the recipient RF-One found for each. It is for inspection only: nothing is delivered to anyone from this page, and it is not the inbox where people handle their requests.

Filters

All, OPEN, ACKNOWLEDGED, RESOLVED, CANCELLED
Show all items, or only the items in that status. OPEN: not yet handled. ACKNOWLEDGED: someone has taken note. RESOLVED: someone acted on it. CANCELLED: the Process that raised it withdrew it.

Columns

Priority
CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW.
Status
One of the statuses above.
Source
The Domain and Process that raised the item, with the phase when given.
Reason
Why the item was raised (first characters). Click it to open the item.
Resolved recipient
The person RF-One found as recipient at the latest routing, or "-".
Routing
resolved: a recipient was found. UNRESOLVED: routing could not find anyone. not routed: routing has not been evaluated yet.
