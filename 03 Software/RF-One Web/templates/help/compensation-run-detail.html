Compensation run

The working page of one preparation run: it shows the hours worked in the period by everyone with Clover Shifts at a Location of this Legal Entity, and lets you calculate each Employee's pay, then mark the run as calculated and approve it.

What you can do

  • Check the hours of each Employee and what is missing before pay can be calculated.
  • Add or remove Incentive Contributions for an Employee.
  • Calculate or recalculate an Employee's pay.
  • Mark the run as Calculated and then Approve it.

Calculations, contributions and the buttons that change the run are available only while the run is OPEN or CALCULATED. After approval the page is read-only and links to the Approved Compensation Snapshot.

Sections of the page

To complete before approving
How many Employees are not yet calculated and how many are SALARIED. Approving includes only the Employees already calculated; anyone left incomplete is excluded from the snapshot, never included with a zero.
Legal Entity / Restaurant problems
Shifts of the period that belong to no Legal Entity, so no run can count them. The Restaurant or Location must be linked to its Legal Entity; RF-One never guesses it.
Hours worked in the period
One row per person: Regular Hours (the first 40 in each Workweek), Overtime Hours (the rest; the overtime amount is determined by the Payroll Provider), Total Hours, Hourly Wage from the Compensation Term (MISSING when there is none), Bonus, Tips, the Hours and Compensation data status (COMPLETE or INCOMPLETE) and the reasons in Missing / to check. PROVISIONAL means the period has not ended yet and the hours will still grow. Click a name to jump to that Employee's card.
Employee card
The calculated figures (Hours, Regular Pay, Tips, Bonus, Recognized Incentive, Tip Credit Make-Up, Gross Pay), or "Not yet calculated". Gross Pay is Regular Pay plus Tips, Bonus and Recognized Incentive.
Incentive Contributions
Positive or negative amounts, each with a label and an optional note. The Recognized Incentive is the sum of the contributions, never below zero. A change applies only after you recalculate the Employee.
Calculate / Recalculate
Earning lines, each a Compensation Term with its hours and an optional work date, plus Tips, Bonus and Tip Credit Make-Up (leave it blank if unknown: it then shows "To complete (Payroll Provider)"). When the person has no HOURLY Compensation Term for this Legal Entity and period, pay cannot be calculated and the form is not shown.
Calculated, but no Shift hours in this period
Calculations saved earlier for people with no supporting Shift in the period. They would still be part of an approval, so they are shown.
SALARIED Employees — not supported in V1
Employees with a SALARIED Compensation Term. Their pay is prepared and communicated to the Payroll Provider outside RF-One; they are never calculated or approved here.

Buttons and what happens

Add Contribution / Remove
Saves or deletes one Incentive Contribution for the Employee. Recalculate the Employee to apply it.
Calculate / Recalculate
Saves the Employee's calculation, replacing the previous one for this run. At least one earning line (Compensation Term and hours) is required.
Mark as Calculated
Shown while the run is OPEN. Moves it to CALCULATED, ready for review. At least one Employee must already be calculated. You can still recalculate afterwards.
Approve
Shown while the run is CALCULATED. After a confirmation, creates the immutable Approved Compensation Snapshot and opens it. This cannot be undone: the run and its calculations can no longer be changed.

Next steps

After approval, continue on the Approved Compensation Snapshot: download the export, record the communication to the Payroll Provider and, later, its result.
