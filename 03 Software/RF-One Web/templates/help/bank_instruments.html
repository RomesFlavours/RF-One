To add an instrument enter institution, display name, type, last four digits or masked identifier and company. Do not enter complete account numbers. Saved source rules can be reassigned, enabled or disabled; the choice is reused on later imports. Inspect assignment history. Recompute accounting deduplication updates accounting links and copy exclusions through the existing service; it does not delete original transactions or initiate payments.
