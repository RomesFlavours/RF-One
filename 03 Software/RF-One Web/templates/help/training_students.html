Student directory

Use New student to enter the full name, email, optional phone and username. Set and confirm an initial password of at least 8 characters. The student must replace it at first sign-in. Saved passwords cannot be retrieved. This page does not send email.

Manage a student

Manage opens the student's form without leaving the list. Save student records changes. Leave both password fields empty to keep the current password. Setting a new password revokes earlier sessions and requires the student to replace it. Cancel or × closes without saving. Help preserves your entries.

Archive student disables access and excludes the student from new assignments, retaining details, assignments and results. Open Archive, choose Manage and Restore student to reactivate access. Administrators and accounts enabled for other domains cannot have their credentials managed here.

Assign content to the student

Choose Assign content on the person's row. The modal shows the student's name; the recipient is fixed. Select one or more available items using the checkboxes and Confirm assignment. Draft and withdrawn courses are excluded. Confirmation assigns each course's currently available version.

Confirmation is disabled with no selection. Archived or inactive students cannot receive new assignments. Errors keep the modal open and preserve selections. ? opens this exact explanation. Cancel or × closes without assigning. After saving, the same student row, search, position and focus are restored. Refresh does not repeat a submission; a new confirmation creates a new path even for content previously assigned.
