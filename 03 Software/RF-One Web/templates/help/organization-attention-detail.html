Attention Item

This page shows one Attention Item: what raised it, why, who RF-One found as its recipient, and every routing evaluation made so far.{% if not may_admin %} You can consult it; actions are reserved to the administrators of the Organization Domain.{% endif %}

Fields

Priority and Status
Priority is CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW. Status is OPEN, ACKNOWLEDGED, RESOLVED or CANCELLED.
Source
The Domain, module, Process and phase that raised the item, and its reference when given.
Reason, Proposed action, Detail
What the Process reported, and what it suggests doing.
Routing
The latest result: the Resolved recipient with the owning Position, or UNRESOLVED with the reason nobody could be found, or Not yet routed.
Routing history (audit trail)
Every evaluation, with its time (in the Location's local time), the owner Position, the recipient, the Resolution path (DIRECT_OCCUPANT, TEMPORARY_COVERAGE, BACKUP_POSITION or ORGANIZATIONAL_FALLBACK) and, when unresolved, the reason. Earlier evaluations are never changed.

Buttons and what happens

Re-evaluate routing now
Looks for the recipient again with the organization as it is now, and adds a new row to the routing history. It does not change the item's status and never rewrites earlier results. Changes to Positions or occupants are not applied to existing items by themselves: use this button after fixing the organization.
Acknowledge
Shown while the item is OPEN or ACKNOWLEDGED, and usable only while it is OPEN. Choose, in the list beside it, the person on whose behalf you record it; the item becomes ACKNOWLEDGED.
Resolve
Choose the person on whose behalf you record it; the item becomes RESOLVED. A resolved item cannot be reopened from this page.

The person and time of each action are recorded with the item.
