Interview

This is an interview someone asked you to give. You speak with an AI interviewer that asks one question at a time about the purpose shown under What we would like to learn. Your answers and the summary are available to the person who requested the interview and to RF-One's Organization administrators.

How to answer

  1. Read What we would like to learn.
  2. Tick I agree and press Start interview. Nothing is sent to the AI until you agree.
  3. Read or listen to the Current question.
  4. Answer in Your answer, by typing or with Speak. Review the text, then press Send answer.
  5. When you have answered at least one question, press Complete interview.

Buttons and what happens

Start interview
Records your agreement and starts the conversation. Your answers are saved in RF-One and used to build the company's know-how; administrators can delete them.
Read questions aloud
When ticked, each new question is read aloud. Listen to the question reads the current one again and Stop speaking stops it.
Speak / Stop dictation
Dictates your answer into the text box. Your browser may send the audio to its speech recognition service; RF-One saves only the text you confirm and send, not the audio.
Send answer
Saves your answer and asks the AI for the next question. A sent answer cannot be edited.
Pause and return to MyRFOne
Leaves the interview. Answers already sent are saved; you can resume from your MyRFOne home page.
Complete interview
Ends the interview. The AI prepares the results for the person who requested it, and you can no longer add answers.

Good to know

  • Conversation history shows the questions and answers so far.
  • If the interview started before agreement was asked, tick I agree with your next answer to continue.
  • If the page says the interview has changed, it was updated in another tab: reload the page and continue.
