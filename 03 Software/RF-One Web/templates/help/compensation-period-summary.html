Period Summary

Shows, for one Location and a range of Business Dates, the hours each Employee worked, the hours above the weekly overtime threshold and the finalized Tips + Gratuity owed to them, so you can compare them with figures prepared by hand. The page is read-only: nothing is calculated into pay, approved or sent.

How to use it

  1. Choose the Location, the First Business Date and the Last Business Date (included).
  2. Press Show summary.
  3. Read the warnings first: if the hours are not definitive, the totals may still change.

What the summary shows

Settings used
The Location, the Business Dates, the timezone, the operating-day cutoff (each Business Date runs from the cutoff to the next day's cutoff), the Workweek, how overtime hours are counted, how Shifts without a Location of their own were attributed, and which FINAL Tips runs supplied the Tips figures (each one links to its saved Tips period).
Employee
Each person with Shifts or a finalized Tips entitlement in the period. check shifts marks a person whose Shifts have a problem; home Location marks Shifts counted here because this is the Employee's home Location.
Worked hours
All hours worked, overtime hours included.
Overtime hours
Hours past the weekly threshold in each Workweek, counted in chronological order. Weeks are never netted against each other. Shown as n/a when the Workweek is not configured.
Tips + Gratuity
The amount due to the person after distribution, taken only from FINAL Tips periods. "not available" when no FINAL period covers these dates.
Bonus
Not defined yet: "to be defined" is not a zero.
Shift problems
The list of Shifts that make the hours incomplete, with the local start time and the problem found.

Next steps

Fix Shift problems at the source, or make the Tips period FINAL in Tips, then show the summary again. To prepare actual pay, use a preparation run from the Compensation home.
