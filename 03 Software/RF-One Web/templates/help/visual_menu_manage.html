Dish cards starts with all Kitchen dish recipes. Choose a restaurant to show the dishes linked to its menus, then choose one of that restaurant's menus or All menus. Active, draft and archived menus can be consulted here. All Kitchen dishes restores the full recipe list for the selected restaurant, including dishes not yet on a menu. Changing restaurant resets the menu filter. Dishes appearing in more than one menu are listed once; labels and preparations are not dish recipes.

Select a restaurant before editing phrases; without one, the list is for browsing and phrase counts are not shown. Select Edit on a dish to manage its restaurant-specific presentation and sales phrases. A restaurant without menus has an empty All menus list; choose All Kitchen dishes to prepare future content. The menu filter changes only the visible list, never Kitchen menu contents.

Enter one presentation or sales phrase per line. Save phrases updates this restaurant's card; it does not change another restaurant's phrases, the recipe or the photograph. Blank text clears that phrase list. Existing phrases are retained and used by Visual Menu when the recipe appears in that restaurant's active menu.

The Kitchen photograph is shown for reference and cannot be edited here. Components, allergens, nutrition, recipe descriptions and characteristics continue to come directly from Kitchen. They are not copied into this page's editable content.

Cancel, × or Escape discards the draft. Saving errors keep the editor and your text open. If someone else saved first, your draft stays available: close and reopen the card to review the latest text before applying changes. Closing the editor returns to its original row and page position. The ? button opens this explanation without discarding your text.

The Dish list tab opens read-only consultation; the Content management tab returns to this workspace. In Training Content, the Visual Menu row’s Contenuti button opens this management page; Descrizione opens the course information modal. The course itself opens the read-only dish list for consultation, and each dish opens its card. MyRF also opens read-only consultation. Student and server accounts cannot open or save through these management endpoints.
