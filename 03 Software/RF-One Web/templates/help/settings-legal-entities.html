Legal Entities

A Legal Entity is a company of the group, identified by its legal name. This page lists the Legal Entities known to RF-One and is the one place where they are created and edited; other parts of RF-One only choose from this list. Only RF-One administrators can open it.

What you can do

  • See every Legal Entity with its status.
  • Create a new Legal Entity or edit an existing one.

Fields and columns

Legal Name
The company's legal name.
Status
ACTIVE or INACTIVE. Where RF-One asks you to choose a Legal Entity for new work, it normally offers only the active ones; what was already recorded with an inactive entity stays as it is.

Buttons and what happens

New Legal Entity
Opens the form to add one. Nothing is saved until you submit the form.
Edit
Opens the form for that Legal Entity, to change its legal name or status.

Legal Entities are never deleted from this page; set one to Inactive when it should no longer be chosen.
