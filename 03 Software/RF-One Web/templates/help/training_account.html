Account activation and recovery

New students activate through the personal email link. Existing unverified accounts receive a six-digit code, valid once for 10 minutes. Enter username, code and a new password twice to verify the email and set the shared RF-One password.

For password recovery, enter username and verified email. The response does not disclose whether an account exists. Enter the emailed code and a new password of at least eight characters twice. Codes expire after 10 minutes or five failed attempts. Changing the password revokes old sessions. Never-activated students ask their trainer to resend the invitation.
