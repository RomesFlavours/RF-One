Interview results

Shows one interview: its purpose, who was interviewed, who requested it, when, and its status. Once the person has completed it, the page shows what the AI proposed from the answers, with the original answers to check them against. While it is not completed, the page shows the request and says that results will be available later.

What the page shows

Interviewee, Requested by, Requested on, Completed on / Progress
Who answers, who asked, and when, in the local time shown on the page.
Summary
The AI's summary of the conversation. It is AI-generated and not validated.
Candidate knowledge
Pieces of know-how the AI proposes, each with the supporting quotation and a View the original answer link. Proposals whose quotation was not found in the answers, or was too short to support them, are left out, and the page says how many.
Open questions
Topics the AI suggests exploring further. An empty list does not mean every relevant topic was covered.
Full conversation
Every question and answer, in order. Open it to check a quotation in context.
Consent not recorded
The interview started before participants were asked to agree. Until the participant agrees with their next answer, its answers and results are visible only to the person who requested it.

These results are proposals, not validated knowledge: read them against the original answers before relying on them.
