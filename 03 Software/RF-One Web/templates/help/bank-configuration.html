Bank Configuration

The lists used to explain every transaction: WHAT (accounting categories), WHY (business reasons), WHO (counterparties) and the entities that can benefit from a transaction. The links at the top jump to each section; Review Transactions goes back to the review.

Sections

1 WHAT
Code, Name, Group and whether it is active. + Add WHAT needs a code, a name and a group; the code of an existing WHAT never changes.
2 WHY
Each WHY with its description and associated WHAT. + Add WHY asks for a name, an optional description and exactly one WHAT. A WHY created from Review without a WHAT can be edited and saved without choosing one; once given, a WHAT cannot be removed. A WHY that settles on a Balance Sheet destination keeps it unless you choose a P&L WHAT.
3 WHO
Each counterparty with its default WHY, how many possible WHY and served entities it has, and whether it is active. Search WHO by name filters the list as you type. In the WHO window you choose the Possible WHY, the Default WHY (only among the possible ones; with a single possible WHY it is the default) and the Entities / LLCs served.
4 Sources
Manage Sources opens the Source tab, where accounts and cards are maintained.
Support
Entities / LLCs: the entities used by “entities served” and For Whom; leave the legal name empty for a virtual (non-LLC) entity. Balance Sheet destinations: read only, with how many WHY use each. Maintained elsewhere says where file recognition, deduplication and completeness control are kept.

Buttons and what happens

+ Add … / Edit
Open the window of that item. Save stores it and the page reloads on the same section; if something is refused the reason is shown and nothing is saved (for a WHO or a WHY your values stay in the window). Cancel or Escape close without saving. Unticking Active hides the item from new choices; nothing is deleted.
Merge (WHO)
Opens Merge WHO: choose the WHO to absorb and the WHO to keep (Swap exchanges them). A preview shows how many transactions, decisions, aliases, possible WHY, entities and supplier links move, and asks you to choose where two settings cannot both survive. Merge moves everything to the kept WHO; the absorbed WHO becomes inactive and its name becomes an alias.

Next steps

Changes are used at once by Review Transactions and AI Rules. A transaction already classified keeps its recorded Who, Why and What until it is reclassified or changed.
