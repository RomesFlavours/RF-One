Your training

This is your training home. Your courses are here as cards, each with its progress and one button: Start, Continue or Review. Your progress saves as you go, so you can stop and come back any time, on any phone.

The Server Course opens with the AI Trainer. Visual Menu teaches you every dish. The dish guide is there whenever you need to look something up, even during a shift.

The final quiz unlocks once you finish all your courses. Your past results stay listed under it. Log out when you're done on a shared device; next time, sign in with the same username and password.
