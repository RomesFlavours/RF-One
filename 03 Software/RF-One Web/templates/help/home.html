Moving around RF-One

  • Every page shows its path at the top (for example RF-One › Domain › Page). Click any level of the path to go back to it; click RF-One or the Rome's Flavours header to return here.
  • Every page has a How … works link at the top right. On a Domain's first page it explains the whole Domain; on every other page it explains that page: what it is for, its fields and buttons, and what happens when you use them.
  • Some Domains open in their own application, in the same window. You stay signed in with the same RF-One account.

If something is missing

If you need a Domain or tool that does not appear here, ask an RF-One administrator to enable it for your account. Log out, at the top right, ends your session on this device.
