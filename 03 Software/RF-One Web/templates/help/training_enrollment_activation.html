Activate Training

Open your personal invitation link. It works once for 48 hours; opening it alone neither creates an account nor signs you in. Review or complete your first name, surname and phone. The invited email cannot be changed here; ask your trainer for a new invitation to change it.

If new, choose and confirm a password of at least eight characters. RF-One generates your username and shows it after activation. If you already have an RF-One account, confirm the current password. Existing credentials and other access are retained. After five incorrect attempts ask for a new invitation.

Confirm and activate Training completes the necessary links and opens Training. Enrollment does not automatically make you an employee. Archived/inactive accounts, identity conflicts, withdrawn Selection confirmation or revoked issuer authorization must be reviewed by the trainer before activation can proceed.
