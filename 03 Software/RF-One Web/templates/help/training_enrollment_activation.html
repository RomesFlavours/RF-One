Getting started

Your invitation email has a personal link. It works once and is good for 48 hours. Opening it doesn't create anything until you press Start my training.

Check your first name, last name and phone. Your email can't be changed here: if it's wrong, ask your manager for a new invitation.

New here? Create a password of at least 8 characters and type it again. We create your username for you and show it on the next screen, so save it somewhere.

Already have an RF-One account? Enter your current password instead. Your username, password and access stay the same. After five wrong tries you'll need a new invitation.

If something doesn't match, for example your details belong to another person in RF-One, your manager needs to check it before you can continue.
