Reconciliation

Compares, component by component, the approved compensation of each Employee with what the Payroll Provider reported, so that every difference is explained or accepted.

Columns

Employee, Component
Who and which part of pay is compared (for example Regular, Tips, Bonus/Incentive or the other amount you recorded).
RF-One (approved) / Provider (reported)
The two values; a dash means that side has no value for this component.
Status
MATCH when the two values agree, DIFFERENT when they do not, MISSING_IN_PROVIDER or MISSING_IN_RFONE when only one side has the component.
Resolution
OPEN (not yet handled), EXPLAINED or ACCEPTED, with the person who set it.
Note
The explanation recorded for the line.

Buttons and what happens

Save
Saves the explanation you typed and, if you choose one, the new resolution (OPEN, EXPLAINED or ACCEPTED) for that line only, recording you as the person who resolved it. "Leave as …" keeps the current resolution. You can change a line again later. The approved figures and the Provider's figures are never changed here.

Next steps

Go through every line still OPEN and explain or accept it. Use the path at the top to return to the Approved Snapshot or the run.
