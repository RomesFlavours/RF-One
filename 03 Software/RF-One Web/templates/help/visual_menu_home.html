Visual Menu

The tab bar follows the RF-One layout: Content management is reserved for trainers; Dish list opens consultation. The active tab is underlined. The contextual help link is on the right.

Visual Menu shows only restaurant locations with an active Kitchen menu, with dishes grouped under its Menu Areas in menu order. Locations without an active menu are hidden; drafts and archived menus are never substituted. If no location has an active menu, the page shows No active menus available.

Choose a linked dish to open its photograph, components, allergens, nutrition and presentation phrases. Text labels remain labels and do not open dish cards. Data and current photographs come directly from Kitchen recipes, without copies. Reload after a menu activation or recipe change to see the current information; links to dishes no longer in the active menu stop working.

Trainers maintain presentation and sales phrases for each restaurant's recipe; existing phrases are retained. Trainees consult their assigned Visual Menu course in Training; servers consult through MyRF. These two views are read-only. Visual Menu stays an independent module and does not require Kitchen access.

If the linked recipe has no photograph, its tile states that the photograph is not yet available. The displayed photo always belongs to the linked recipe, never to a different dish sharing its label.
