How Development Journal works

The Development Journal keeps a written record of each piece of development work, from the first study to the release. Each piece of work has one card; on the card the people involved add dated entries, attach documents and keep the next step up to date, so anyone can see where the work stands and why.

How it works

  • A work is one change with a verifiable result. It has a title, the RF-One Domain it concerns, a responsible person, an objective, a Phase (Study, Specification, Development, Local test, Ready for release, Released) and a Status (In progress, Waiting, Blocked, Completed, Cancelled).
  • Entries are the journal of the work: analyses, decisions, activities, verifications, problems and releases. Date and author are recorded automatically.
  • Nothing is lost: every phase or status change is kept in the card's history, and a corrected entry keeps its earlier versions. Works and entries cannot be deleted.
  • Everything is written by hand. The journal does not read Git, Claude, Codex or AWS by itself, and it does not send notifications.

Typical flow

  1. Click New work, describe the objective, choose the Domain and the responsible person, and create the card.
  2. Open the card and add entries as the work progresses (analysis, decisions, activities, verifications, problems).
  3. Attach the useful documents (specifications, screenshots, reports) to the card or to a single entry.
  4. With Edit on the card, move the work to the next phase, change its status and keep the Next step current.
  5. When the work is released, add a Release entry with environment, service, version and outcome, then set the status to Completed.

What this page shows

The list of all works, with filters.

Domain, Phase, Status, Responsible
Filters. Choose one or more values and click Filter; Clear shows every work again.
Title
Click it to open the work's card.
Last update
When the card or one of its entries last changed, in the local time shown under the table.
Next step
What has to be done now. “to be defined” means an open work has no next step yet: set it from the card with Edit.

Buttons and what happens

New work
Opens the form to create a new card. Nothing is saved until you click Create work there.
Filter / Clear
Only change what the list shows; they never change any data.
