Accounts

This page lists every RF-One account: the people who can sign in to RF-One. Only RF-One administrators can open it. From here you create new accounts and open the pages that change an existing account, its Domain access, its password and its Training identity.

What you can do

  • See at a glance who has an account, whether it is active and whether it is an administrator.
  • Create a new account.
  • Open one account to change its name, status or administrator role, to reset its password, to choose the Domains it may use, or to link it to a Training identity.

Fields and columns

Display name
The name shown for the person inside RF-One.
Username
The name the person types to sign in. It is stored in lower case and cannot be changed after the account is created.
Status
ACTIVE accounts can sign in; INACTIVE accounts are refused at sign-in with the message that the account is not active. Nothing about the account is deleted.
Admin
"Owner" is the owner of RF-One, who always stays an active administrator. "Yes" marks any other RF-One administrator. Empty means a normal account.

Buttons and what happens

Create account
Opens the form for a new account. Nothing is saved until you submit that form.
Edit
Opens the account's form, where you change its display name, status and administrator role.
Domain Access
Opens the page where you decide which Domains (and which sections of them) this account may use, and with which role.
Reset Password
Opens the same Edit page, scrolled to its Reset password box.
Training Identity
Opens the page that links this RF-One account to its identity in Training.

Next steps

After creating an account, open Domain Access for it: a new account sees no Domain on its Home until you grant one. Accounts are never deleted from this page; to stop someone signing in, set the account to Inactive.
