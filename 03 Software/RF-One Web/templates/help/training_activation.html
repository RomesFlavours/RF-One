Activate your account

Open the personal link emailed after registration. It works once for 48 hours and alone never grants access. First enter the username and initial password from the invitation and select Verify. Usernames are case-insensitive. Then choose and confirm a different personal password of at least eight characters and select Activate and continue.

Completion verifies your email, invalidates the link and initial password, and opens MyRF with your authorized modules, including Training. You cannot enter MyRF or modules before completion. If the link is invalid, used or expired, ask the trainer to resend the invitation. After activation use Forgot password on the sign-in page for recovery.
