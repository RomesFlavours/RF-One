How Anagrafica dipendenti works

The Anagrafica dipendenti (employee directory) is RF-One's one register of people: every person has a single RF-One identity, used by all modules, and every employee has one RF-One account. Clover is a source of observations: RF-One reads the Clover employee lists and clock-ins of each site and never writes to Clover. Accounts of external people (for example consultants) are managed in Accounts, not here.

How it works

  • A person can hold a position at several sites, even with more than one Clover account at the same site.
  • Only a complete and successful reading of a site's Clover employee list and clock-ins can close a position. When a person disappears from that list, only that site's position ends. When no active position is left, the person moves to Dismissis, with the last clock-in as the end date, and their RF-One access is blocked.
  • Failed or incomplete readings, sites that are not configured and Clover identities still waiting to be reconciled never end anyone's employment. A person who reappears in Clover becomes active again, but their access is not reactivated automatically.
  • Every new Clover identity with at least one clock-in waits in Collegamenti da riconciliare until a directory administrator confirms who it is. RF-One never merges people by similar name or e-mail; its suggestions are only suggestions. Until confirmed, that identity's orders, payments and Shifts stay on hold.

Typical flow

  1. Check the last synchronization of each site{% if can_sync %} and press Sincronizza ora if you need fresh data{% endif %}.
  2. Review Collegamenti da riconciliare{% if can_manage %}: link each new Clover identity to an existing person or confirm it is a new person{% endif %}.
  3. Look at Da verificare in the list of active employees and open the person to resolve it.
  4. Open a person to see and{% if not can_manage %}, with directory administration rights,{% endif %} edit their record, organizational applicability and links.

What this page shows

Ultima sincronizzazione
For each site, when its Clover data was last read, in the site's local time. A Clover service also reads the employee list about every minute.
Dipendenti attivi
Active employees grouped by site, with Applicabilità (where the person works: one restaurant, several, the whole group, or not configured), Ultimo aggiornamento (last time Clover reported them), Account RF1 (state of their account) and Da verificare (differences between POS data, absence from Clover with the end still undecided, a site not yet verified, or a position ended at another site).
Dismissis
Closed by default. People with no active position, with their end date and positions. Their history, payments, Shifts and documents stay unchanged, but they cannot be selected for new operations.
{% if technical %}
Osservazioni tecniche
Clover names without any verified clock-in or clock-out. They are not employees, get no RF-One account and stay here only so that historical data can be attributed.
{% endif %}
Collegamenti da riconciliare
New or no-longer-present Clover identities waiting for confirmation, with their e-mail, last clock-in and possible matches.

Buttons and what happens

Cerca
Filters the people by name, e-mail or code. It changes nothing.
{% if can_sync %}
Sincronizza ora
Starts a Clover reading of that site straight away. Refresh the page to see the outcome.
{% endif %} {% if can_manage %}
Nuova persona RF1 → Crea persona in RF1
Creates a person without a POS and without a site (Nome completo required, Email and Telefono optional) and opens their record. An RF-One account is prepared for them, INACTIVE, with no credentials and no permissions: link an existing account in the record or activate it from Accounts.
Collega alla persona
Links the Clover identity to the person chosen in Persona RF-One. Its held work records are then attributed to that person, and the decision is recorded in the person's history. Run a synchronization to recover any pending work records.
Conferma che è una persona nuova
Confirms that the Clover identity is a new person and creates them in the directory.
