The work card

This page is the complete record of one work: its objective and current state, its documents, its journal of entries and the history of its phase and status changes. Here you add entries and documents as the work progresses.

What you can do

  • Read the Next step and the current phase and status at the top.
  • Add a new entry to the journal, with optional documents.
  • Attach documents to the card or to a single entry.
  • Change the card (title, Domain, responsible, phase, status, objective, next step) with Edit{% if work is defined and closed_statuses is defined and work.status in closed_statuses %}, or reopen it with Reopen{% endif %}.
  • Correct an entry, when you are allowed to, and look at the earlier versions of a corrected entry.

Sections of the page

Next step
What has to be done now, and by whom. A completed or cancelled work has none.
Card
Objective, Domain, Responsible, Phase, Status, who created the card and who last updated it, with the time.
Card documents
Documents attached to the work as a whole. Click a name to open it in a new tab, or download to save it. Accepted: PDF, images, Office files, TXT and Markdown{% if max_document_mb is defined %}, up to {{ max_document_mb }} MB per file{% endif %}. Documents are never public: only people with access to the Development Journal can open them.
Journal
The entries in the order they were written, oldest first. Each shows its type, date, author and reference phase. A corrected entry shows “corrected … version N · earlier versions”; click it to see every version.
Add entry
The form for a new entry (see below).
Phase and status history
Every phase or status change since the card was created: when, who, from what to what.
Events recorded for MyRF
A log of what happened on the card (new work, entries, corrections, uploads, changes). It is recorded for future signals; no notification is sent yet.

The Add entry form

Reference phase
The phase of the work the entry belongs to. It starts at the current phase. Choosing another phase here does not change the phase of the work.
Type
Analysis, Decision, Activity, Verification, Problem or Release.
Text
Required. Free text; you can also paste a Claude or Codex report.
Release
Shown only for the Release type. Environment (Test or Production), AWS service, Version or commit and Outcome (Succeeded or Failed) are required; Post-deploy verification is optional. A failed release stays documented and changes neither the phase nor the status of the work.
References (optional)
A link (starting with https:// or http://), a branch, a commit and the command or prompt used.
Documents (optional)
Files attached to this entry when it is saved.

Buttons and what happens

Add entry
Moves you to the Add entry form at the bottom of the page.
Save entry
Saves the entry with the current date and your name, and attaches the chosen documents. Entries cannot be deleted; they can only be corrected. If something is missing, the entry is not saved and the page shows the reason with your text still in the form.
Edit
Opens the form to change the card. Phase and status changes are kept in the history.
{% if work is defined and closed_statuses is defined and work.status in closed_statuses %}
Reopen
Shown only on a completed or cancelled work. It sets the status back to In progress immediately and records the change in the history.
{% endif %}
Upload
Uploads the chosen files to the card (or, under “Attach documents to this entry”, to that entry). Uploaded documents cannot be removed from this page.
Correct
Shown next to the entries you are allowed to correct (normally the ones you wrote). Opens the correction page; the current text is kept as an earlier version.

Next steps

Keep the Next step current with Edit each time the work moves on. When the work is released, add a Release entry, then set the status to Completed.
