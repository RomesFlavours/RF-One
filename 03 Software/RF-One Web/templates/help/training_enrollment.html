Invite a student

From Students select Invite student. When Selection marks a candidate as Hired, it releases them to Training and a trainer is put in charge: nothing else happens yet. Choose «Released by Selection: confirm arrival» and the candidate released to you, or send a direct invitation using an email and any known name and phone number. Available CV details are prefilled. Check the address before creating the invitation. Creating the invitation for a released candidate is the arrival confirmation: only then is the person registered as a Student in the Registry and enrolled in the school. Only the trainer the candidate was released to can confirm the arrival. Candidates of a Selection position you may manage are listed too; one not yet released is marked «not released to Training yet» and its arrival cannot be confirmed until Selection releases it. Pressing it again for a candidate whose invitation is pending or already used changes nothing. The invitation belongs to the person: no site is needed, and none is assigned. Optionally choose the Role the person trains for from the Registry role catalog (roles are common to every site); it is noted on the Registry card, not as a work assignment. Creating the invitation creates the person's Registry card, or reuses the existing card of the same person, with status Trainee and no site; an Employee keeps the status. Work sites (WP, MD) are assigned later on the Registry card. The invitation gives no permission beyond Training. Repeating it reuses the same identity and card. No hiring decision or Clover record is created; the POS ID is added later on the Registry card.

Before anything is created, RF-One looks for an existing Registry card with the same email or phone. One card with the same email is reused. When the match is uncertain (same phone only, several cards, or a different phone) the page asks «Is this the same person as an existing Registry card?»: choose the card, or «A different person: create a new card». A US number counts as the same phone with or without +1. Your choice is checked again when you submit: if the matching cards changed in the meantime, nothing is created, the page says «The Registry match changed» and shows the updated options to choose again. Until you choose, nothing is saved: no arrival, Trainee status, card or invitation. A card that belongs to another person is never reused. Possible Registry matches by name, email or phone require explicit confirmation. Stop and request a Registry review when this is not the same person. Records are never automatically merged or duplicated to bypass a conflict.

Create invitation emails a personal link that works once and expires after 48 hours. If delivery fails, copy the link or create another after one minute. Reissue replaces earlier pending invitations for the same email; at most five may be issued per hour. The Registry person and card exist from the invitation; the login and Training account are created at activation.

Both routes use the same activation page: the recipient confirms details and chooses a password when new. Existing RF-One account holders confirm their current password, preserving credentials and other access. Selection confirmation, the arrival confirmation and the issuer’s authorization are checked again during activation; a link issued before the arrival was confirmed no longer works. Full manual student entry remains available under New student.
