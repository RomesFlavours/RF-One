Invite a student

From Students select Invite student. Choose a currently confirmed Selection candidate from a position you may manage, or send a direct invitation using an email and any known name and phone number. Available CV details are prefilled. Check the address before creating the invitation. No employee record or hiring decision is created.

Possible Registry matches by name, email or phone require explicit confirmation. Stop and request a Registry review when this is not the same person. Records are never automatically merged or duplicated to bypass a conflict.

Create invitation emails a personal link that works once and expires after 48 hours. If delivery fails, copy the link or create another after one minute. Reissue replaces earlier pending invitations for the same email; at most five may be issued per hour. No person/login is created before activation.

Both routes use the same activation page: the recipient confirms details and chooses a password when new. Existing RF-One account holders confirm their current password, preserving credentials and other access. Selection confirmation and the issuer’s authorization are checked again during activation. Full manual student entry remains available under New student.
