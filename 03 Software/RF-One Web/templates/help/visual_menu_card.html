Visual Menu

The tab bar follows the RF-One layout: Content management is reserved for trainers; Dish list opens consultation. The active tab is underlined. The contextual help link is on the right.

This card is available only while its dish is in the active menu for this location. The menu name and Area identify its current grouping. The photograph appears first: compact on desktop and full content width on tablets and phones. Check the restaurant and dish name beside or below it.

Read the sections in order: Components, Allergens, Nutritional profile, Dish description, Characteristics and curiosities, then sales phrases. Components appear one per line without quantities. A preparation is shown as a component; its ingredients and any nested preparations are also checked for allergens.

Allergens are listed individually, including those identified in linked preparations. An unlisted allergen is not confirmed absent, and cross-contact precautions still require kitchen confirmation.

Nutritional figures are preliminary recipe totals, not validated per serving. Missing descriptions, characteristics and nutritional information are shown as unavailable, never invented.

Recipes supplies components, photographs, nutrition and sensory details live. Reload to see recipe changes; Visual Menu keeps no copies. Other-location links appear only where the same recipe is also in that location's active menu. Labels do not have recipe cards.

This is a read-only card for consultation, including when previewed by a trainer. Presentation and sales phrases are maintained on the separate Dish cards management page.

Consultation does not certify competence or complete an assessment. Local preview access is automatic; shared access follows the normal role permissions.
