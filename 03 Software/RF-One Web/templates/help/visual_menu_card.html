Visual Menu

The photograph appears first: compact on desktop and full content width on tablets and phones. Check the restaurant and dish name beside or below it.

Read the sections in order: Components, Allergens, Nutritional profile, Dish description, Characteristics and curiosities, then sales phrases. Components appear one per line without quantities. A preparation is shown as a component; its ingredients and any nested preparations are also checked for allergens.

Allergens are listed individually, including those identified in linked preparations. An unlisted allergen is not confirmed absent, and cross-contact precautions still require kitchen confirmation.

Nutritional figures are preliminary recipe totals, not validated per serving. Missing descriptions, characteristics and nutritional information are shown as unavailable, never invented.

Recipes supplies components, photographs, nutrition and sensory details live. Reload to see recipe changes; Visual Menu keeps no copies. The other-restaurant link opens that restaurant’s own card.
