Organizational Fallback Policy

The Fallback Policy names the Position that receives an Attention Item as a last resort, when no owner, Temporary Coverage or Backup Position can be found for it. It is a choice of your company, not a fixed rule of RF-One: without a policy, such items simply stay unresolved. A typical choice is the CEO Position with scope GLOBAL.

Columns

Scope
Where the policy applies. GLOBAL applies everywhere; a more specific policy that matches a request wins over the GLOBAL one, and among equally specific ones the most recently created wins.
Fallback Position
The Position that receives the requests{% if may_use_item('ORGANIZATION', 'positions') %}; click it to open the Position{% endif %}. The request reaches its occupant, or the delegate of its active Temporary Coverage; if that Position is vacant too, the request stays unresolved.
Active
Only active policies are used.

Buttons and what happens

Set Fallback Policy
Adds the policy and reloads the page. It does not replace an existing one: an older policy with the same scope stays active, and the newer one wins. If the scope value is missing or wrong, nothing is saved and the reason is shown.
Deactivate
Takes that policy out of use. It stays listed as not active and cannot be reactivated from this page; set a new policy instead.
