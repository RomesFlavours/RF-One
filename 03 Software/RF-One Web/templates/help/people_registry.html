This administrative directory includes employees and external people in one Registry. Search by name or email. An account is a linked login; employment is a separate link. A student remains in the central Registry without automatically becoming an employee.

Training creation assigns the explicit STUDENT qualification independently of permissions. Bank and Organization operational choices exclude students without explicit employee links; this administrative directory retains them. Open a person to inspect login and employment links. Link a source only after confirming it belongs to that person; a similar name is not identity proof.

This changes neither roles, permissions nor account status. Every employee is eligible for courses without a separate STUDENT qualification; an active login and Training access are still required.
