Review transactions — Reconciled

This tab lists the transactions whose WHO is answered, including transfers between the business's own accounts, which need no WHO. Here you finish each one: WHY, For Whom and the final Confirm. Only Confirmed transactions can go into the Monthly Export.

What you can do

  • Narrow the list with Year, Month and Instrument and Filter, or use Search transactions (description, WHO, WHY or Source name; Enter or Search, × clears only the search).
  • Sort by clicking the Account / Card, Date, Description or Amount header; click again to reverse.
  • Switch to To Reconcile with the tabs; filters, search and sorting are kept.
  • After every action you come back to the same row, highlighted.

Fields and columns

Account / Card · For Whom
The Source of the transaction and, under it, For Whom: the company or person that benefited. Choosing it saves it at once. Entities the WHO usually serves are marked ✓. If the transaction was Confirmed, a new For Whom sends it back to Needs review until it is confirmed again.
WHO
The counterparty. ✓ means chosen by a person; “suggested” means recognised automatically and not yet approved. An own-account movement shows its own-account label instead.
WHY and WHAT
The business purpose and the accounting category. “WHY needed” means the WHY is still missing.
Status
✗ Needs review: a person still has to accept it, and it blocks the export. ✔ Confirmed: accepted by a person, ready for the export.

Buttons and what happens

✎ next to WHO, ✎ WHY
Open the Select WHO / WHY window with the row's current WHO and WHY: choose any active WHO and any active WHY (a dot marks the WHY already used with that WHO), or create them with + New WHO and + New WHY. Confirm records them as a person's decision for this transaction only; no rule is created. Cancel closes without saving.
Confirm
Accepts the transaction with its current WHO, WHY and WHAT and the chosen For Whom; the status becomes Confirmed. It is available only when the row has a WHO, a WHY and a For Whom.
Reopen
On a Confirmed row: withdraws the acceptance. The decision and the For Whom are kept; the row goes back to Needs review and needs a new Confirm.
▸ (expand)
Opens the editor under the row: the transaction's facts (account, date, amount, description, memo, where its WHO and accounting came from) and the fields WHO, WHY, WHAT and For whom. Choosing a WHY proposes its WHAT; you can choose another. Save needs all four values and accepts the transaction as Confirmed with exactly those values. Collapse closes the editor without saving. While a Confirmed row is edited and not saved it shows “Changed — not saved”.

Next steps
