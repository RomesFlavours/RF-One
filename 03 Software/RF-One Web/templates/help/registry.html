Registry gestisce persone, aziende, aree, ruoli e autorizzazioni. In Persone e assegnazioni scegli la Sede e premi Mostra: la lista contiene solo le persone con un’assegnazione esplicita a quella sede. L’azienda, i collegamenti Clover e la sede indicata sulla scheda non decidono chi compare. Una persona assegnata a due sedi compare in entrambe con lo stesso ID RF-One. Una sede senza assegnazioni mostra una lista vuota; una sede non disponibile mostra un avviso e nessun’altra sede al suo posto.

Le schede nascono solo in due modi: con Aggiungi persona (indicando sede e ruolo) o da Training (invito diretto o candidato rilasciato da Selection). Clover non crea, non aggiorna e non propone schede. Per aggiungere una sede a una persona già registrata apri la sua Scheda e usa «Aggiungi una sede». Il POS ID per Tips si inserisce sulla Scheda, per ciascuna sede. «Persone senza sede» elenca chi non ha ancora un’assegnazione.

{% else %}

Registry manages people, companies, areas, roles and permissions. In People & assignments choose the Site and select Show: the list contains only the people explicitly assigned to that site. Company membership, Clover links and the site written on the card never decide who is listed. A person assigned to two sites appears at both with the same RF-One ID. A site without assignments shows an empty list; an unavailable site shows a notice and never another site instead.

Cards are created only with Add person (naming the site and role) or by Training (direct invitation or a candidate released by Selection). Clover never creates, updates or proposes a card. To add a site to a person already registered, open their Card and use «Add a site». The POS ID used by Tips is typed on the Card, per site. «People without a site» lists who has no assignment yet.

{% endif %}

A Training entry creates or reuses the person's card, with status Trainee and no site (work sites are assigned here, on the card) (an Employee keeps the status); Confirm hire in Training turns the same card into Employee. No other module permission is granted. Students activate using their personal email link and replace the initial password before accessing RF-One.

Person editor

Enter the person's name and contact details; when adding a person also choose the site and the role there (required: a person with the same email or phone is not created twice). Select companies, and add roles within each company and area. Save changes records the profile. Other system authorizations and Reset password open the linked account in a separate window while retaining this draft. Dismiss person requires a dismissal date and preserves history. Cancel, X and Escape close without saving. Opening and closing Help preserves the draft.
