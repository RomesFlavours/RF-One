Registry gestisce persone, aziende, aree, ruoli e autorizzazioni. Nella scheda Persone trovi anche le identità Clover da confermare.

Controlla nome, sede e turni in attesa. Le ore sono quelle locali della sede. Scegli la persona RF-One corretta e premi Collega alla persona. Crea una persona nuova solo se non esiste già. I suggerimenti non confermano alcun abbinamento.

La conferma attribuisce nella stessa operazione i dati di lavoro in attesa e registra la decisione. Non modifica ruoli o permessi e non approva le mance. Dopo la conferma torna al periodo Tips, ricalcola e finalizza: il totale bilanciato da solo non basta. I collegamenti già confermati non possono essere riassegnati qui.

Registry manages people, companies, areas, roles and permissions. People & assignments also lists Clover identities requiring confirmation.

Review the name, location and held shifts in location time. Choose the correct RF-One person and select Link to person. Confirm a new person only if they do not already exist. Suggestions never confirm a match.

Confirmation attributes held work and records the decision in one transaction. It does not grant permissions or approve tips. Return to the Tips period, recalculate and finalize. Balanced totals alone do not approve a period. Confirmed identities cannot be reassigned here.

New Training students appear as external RF-One accounts in Registry. Their central person identity and Training account are explicitly linked, without creating an employee or granting other modules. Students activate using their personal email link and replace the initial password before accessing RF-One.

Person editor

Enter the person's name and contact details, select companies, and add roles within each company and area. Save changes records the profile. Other system authorizations and Reset password open the linked account in a separate window while retaining this draft. Dismiss person requires a dismissal date and preserves history. Cancel, X and Escape close without saving. Opening and closing Help preserves the draft.
