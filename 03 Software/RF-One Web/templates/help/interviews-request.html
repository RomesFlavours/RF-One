How Interviews works

An interview collects a person's know-how through a conversation with an AI interviewer. You request it and explain what you want to learn; the person answers by voice or text from their MyRFOne home page; you then review the answers and the results the AI proposes. Interviews is a test prototype: AI-generated results require human review.

Typical flow

  1. Choose the Person to interview.
  2. Write the Purpose to explain to the AI: what you would like to learn (up to 4000 characters).
  3. Press Request interview. The person finds the request on their MyRFOne home page.
  4. The person agrees to take part, answers the AI's questions and completes the interview.
  5. Open the interview from All interviews to read the summary, the candidate knowledge, the open questions and the full conversation.

Fields

Person to interview
Only current employees are listed, as the employee directory decides; dismissed people never appear. A person needs one active RF-One account linked to their record to receive the interview: without it the name is shown with "no active linked account" and cannot be chosen. When you choose a person, the page shows their RF-One access{% if interview_directory_access is defined and interview_directory_access %} and an Open person record link{% endif %}.
Purpose to explain to the AI
What the AI should explore with the person. The person reads it before starting.

Buttons and what happens

Request interview
Creates the interview and opens its page. RF-One checks the person again at this moment: if they were dismissed after you opened the page, the request is refused and nothing is created. A request is not sent by e-mail; it appears on the person's MyRFOne home page.
View request / View results
Opens an interview: its request while it is not completed, its results once it is.

All interviews

Domain administrators of Organization see every interview; other people with Interviews access see the interviews they requested. Status shows where each interview is; "Consent not recorded" marks interviews started before participants were asked to agree. Past interviews, answers and results stay available after a person is dismissed. When the page is opened for one person{% if interview_directory_access is defined and interview_directory_access %} (for example from their record){% endif %}, the list shows only that person's interviews; All interviews returns to the full list. If the person is no longer a current employee, their past interviews are shown but a new one cannot be requested.
