Review transactions — To Reconcile

This tab lists the transactions whose WHO is not answered yet, and those with a possible duplicate still to decide. Here you give them their WHO and WHY. As soon as a transaction has its WHO it leaves this tab and appears under Reconciled, where its For Whom and Confirm are completed.

What you can do

  • Narrow the list with Year, Month and Instrument, then Filter. With no month, every month is shown (at most {{ row_limit if row_limit is defined else 500 }} rows; narrow the list when it says so).
  • Search transactions finds a text in the description, the WHO, the WHY or the Source name. Press Enter or Search; × clears only the search. The result line shows how many transactions match and their total.
  • Click the Instrument, Date, Description or Amount header to sort; click again to reverse. Filters and search are kept.
  • Move between To Reconcile and Reconciled with the tabs: month, instrument, search and sorting are kept. The number on each tab is how many transactions it holds.
  • When you arrive from a file's Review issues on Import and Review, only that file's rows are shown; Show every batch removes that limit.

Fields and columns

Tick box
Selects the row for a shared WHO / WHY. Ticking saves nothing. Clear selection removes every tick; changing search, filters or tab clears it too.
Instrument and For Whom
The Source the transaction was imported from (it is set by the import and cannot be changed here). For Whom is the company or person that benefited: choosing it saves it at once and changes nothing else. A split transaction shows “For Whom: split”.
Date, Description, Amount, Source
The facts as they came from the bank file.
Duplicate
“Da verificare” marks a possible duplicate: same date, description and amount as another transaction (linked by its number). It is never removed automatically.
Classification (Who → Why → What)
The status of the current decision and, once resolved, its Who, Why and What. “EXCLUDED FROM ACCOUNTING” marks a copy of the same accounting fact as another transaction: classify that one, not the copy (its Duplicate group shows both). “NO SETTLEMENT ACCOUNT” means the card has no settlement account for that date: fix it on Source.
Internal Transfer
A movement between two of the business's own accounts. When RF-One finds possible counterparts you can choose one in Match with….
Review
The review state of the imported row.

Buttons and what happens

Select WHO
Opens the Select WHO / WHY window for that transaction. On a ticked row the button reads Select WHO / WHY and the window works for every ticked transaction at once.
Select WHO / WHY window
Three columns: WHO (every active WHO, searchable), WHY GROUP (groups that organise the WHY list, with no accounting effect) and WHY (any active WHY; a dot marks the WHY already used with the chosen WHO, and its search covers all groups). Confirm becomes available when a WHO and a WHY are chosen: it records them as a person's decision for this transaction, which then moves to Reconciled. For several ticked transactions it reads Confirm for N transactions and saves all of them or none. Cancel, × or Escape close it without saving and keep the ticks. No recognition rule is created.
+ New WHO
Opens Create New WHO on top: type the WHO Name; similar existing WHO are offered so you can pick one instead. Save creates the WHO (or reuses the active WHO with that name) and selects it; the transaction is classified only when you press Confirm.
+ New WHY
Opens Create New WHY: WHY Name, WHY Group (+ New WHY Group creates a group by name) and an optional WHAT / Accounting destination. Save creates the WHY, links it to the chosen WHO and selects it; Confirm then records it.
Reclassify
On a resolved row: derives the What again from the current mapping of the same Why, as a new recorded decision. The previous decision is kept in the history.
Distinct / Duplicate
Your verdict on a possible duplicate. Distinct keeps it as a separate operation; Duplicate confirms it as a copy, which then leaves the Review and the Monthly Export (the row itself is kept). The accounting deduplication is recomputed at once.
Confirm transfer
Records the chosen counterpart as an internal transfer. Such a movement needs no WHO and moves to Reconciled.

Next steps
