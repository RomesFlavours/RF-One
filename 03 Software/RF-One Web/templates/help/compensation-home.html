How Compensation works

Compensation prepares what each Employee of a Legal Entity is owed for a pay period, lets a person review and approve it, and then records the manual handoff to the Payroll Provider and the Provider's result. RF-One does not send anything to the Payroll Provider by itself: the export and the Provider's result are handled by a person, in both directions.

How it works

  • A preparation run covers one Legal Entity and one period. Everyone with Clover Shifts in that period at a Location of the Legal Entity appears in it, with Regular and Overtime hours per Workweek.
  • Pay is calculated per Employee from their HOURLY Compensation Term, plus Tips, Bonus and Incentive Contributions. SALARIED Employees are listed but not calculated: their pay is prepared outside RF-One.
  • Approving a run creates an Approved Compensation Snapshot that can no longer change. It holds only the Employees already calculated.
  • From the snapshot you download the figures for the Payroll Provider, record that you communicated them, and later record what the Provider reported, which RF-One compares with the approved figures.

Typical flow

  1. Press New preparation run, choose the Legal Entity and the period.
  2. In the run, check the hours, add any Incentive Contributions and press Calculate for each Employee.
  3. Press Mark as Calculated, review, then Approve.
  4. On the Approved Snapshot, Download CSV, enter the figures in the Payroll Provider and press Confirm communication.
  5. When the Provider has processed payroll, enter its values with Record result and reconcile and explain or accept each difference.

What this page shows

Legal Entity, Period, Status
Every preparation run, newest period first. Status is OPEN (being prepared), CALCULATED (ready for review), APPROVED (snapshot created), EXPORTED (communication to the Provider recorded) or CLOSED.
Open
Opens the run with its hours and calculations.
Approved snapshot
Shown once a run is approved: opens its immutable snapshot, export and reconciliation.

Buttons

New preparation run
Opens the form to create a run. Nothing is created until you submit that form.
Period Summary
A read-only comparison of hours, overtime hours and finalized Tips + Gratuity per Employee for one Location. It calculates, approves and sends nothing.
