WHO Rules

WHO is recognised in two ways, in this order — here and in the reconciliation engine alike: first the WHO recognition rules you approve below; then, only where no rule read a WHO, WHO Search Text: the name or an alias of a registered WHO found in the description. Several WHO, or rules that disagree: the movement goes to review. A WHO confirmed by a person is never replaced. Nothing here changes a WHO, WHY or WHAT of a movement.

WHO recognition rules —

Describe the rule to the AI, e.g. “If you find PAYPAL, the WHO is what comes after IND ID: up to IND NAME:”. Check the movements it applies to in the preview, then save. A movement read by a rule is not searched again by names and aliases.

WHO Search Text —

Where no rule read a WHO, the WHO is the one whose name or alias appears in the description as whole words. On any movement, Text saves the text to search: as an alias of an existing WHO, or as the name of a new WHO. Every bank movement is listed with the WHO recognised and how.
