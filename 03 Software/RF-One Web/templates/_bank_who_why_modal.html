Every active WHY can be chosen for any WHO; the WHY already associated with the chosen WHO are only MARKED (and counted per group). The page ships no WHO x WHY matrix: the active WHO list and the grouped WHY catalog are fetched once, on first open; the WHY associated with the ONE chosen WHO when it is chosen. "+ Create New WHY" opens the ONE shared dialog (`_bank_why_create_dialog.html`, also used by the WHO Rule modal); the page including this popup includes that dialog once, after it.
