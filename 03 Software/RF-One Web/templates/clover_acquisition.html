Historical Backfill

Manually recovers or rebuilds one specific historical period — a known gap, a first load, a correction. Not the everyday way to update RF-One (that is Sync Now).

Read-only against Clover — nothing is ever written back. Re-acquiring a period updates the Orders, Payments, Tips, Gratuity and Shifts already in RF-One from Clover's current values; nothing is duplicated.
