Split{% if txn_id %} · movement #{{ txn_id }}{% endif %}

One bank movement stays one movement and may carry several lines, each with its amount, WHY, the WHAT of that WHY and the products or documents behind it. The lines must add up to the movement exactly. Products with the same WHY are one line; different WHYs are different lines. When amounts per product are missing the split stays incomplete: the movement is never divided in equal parts and the prevailing category is never used. WHOM follows the Source owner at the date (or the WHOM chosen for the movement).
