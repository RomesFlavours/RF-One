WHY Rules

Recognising the WHO and determining the WHY are two steps. For a UNIQUE WHO the WHY is the one set on its WHO + Source classification. For a MULTIPLE WHO the WHY depends on the single movement: only a WHO WHY rule decides it — conditions on Source, accounting subject at the date, description, Source information or documents — and a WHY registered on the pair is not used. WHAT always comes from the WHY; WHOM from the Source. Below: the movements whose WHO is recognised but whose WHY cannot be determined, grouped by WHO, Source and reason. Nothing here changes a movement.
