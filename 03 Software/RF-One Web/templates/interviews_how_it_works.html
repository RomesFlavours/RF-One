How Interviews works

An interview collects a person's know-how through a conversation with the AI. A manager requests it, the person answers by text or voice, and the manager reviews the results.

Request an interview · Results · Answer an interview

Request an interview

Open Organization / Interviews. This page needs the Interviews item of the Organization Domain.

  1. Choose the person. The list shows only current employees, as the Employee Directory decides. Dismissed people never appear, including the owner when the owner's person record is dismissed.
  2. Login required. The person needs one active RF-One account linked to their person record. Without it the name is shown but cannot be selected.
  3. Describe the purpose the AI should explore (up to 4000 characters) and press Request interview. The person finds the request on their MyRFOne home page.

RF-One checks the person again when you press Request interview. If they were dismissed after you opened the page, the request is refused and nothing is created. An old person number that was merged into another record counts as that record.

Opening Interviews from the record of a person who is no longer a current employee shows their past interviews, with a notice that a new interview cannot be requested.

All interviews. Domain administrators of Organization see every interview. Other people with Interviews access see the interviews they requested. Past interviews, answers and results stay available after a person is dismissed.

Results

Open an interview from the list to see its purpose, status, interviewee and, once completed, the answers and the AI results. AI-generated results require human review.

Interviews started before participants were asked for consent show Consent not recorded: their answers and results are visible only to the person who requested them until the participant agrees on their next answer.

Domain administrators of Organization can delete an interview by ticking the confirmation box. The interview, its answers and results are removed; the person's directory history keeps who deleted it and when, not its content.

Answer an interview

Open the request from your MyRFOne home page. Read what we would like to learn, tick I agree and press Start interview. Your answers are saved in RF-One and used to build the company's know-how.

Answer each question by typing or with Speak; review the text and press Send answer. You can listen to the question aloud. When you have answered at least one question, press Complete interview.

If the page says the interview has changed, it was updated in another tab: reload the page and continue.
