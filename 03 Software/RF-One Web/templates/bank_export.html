Monthly Accountant Export

One report per Owning Entity of the Sources — a Company or a person — for the accountant: RfBank_YYYY_MM_<Owner>.xlsx. It contains only the movements of that owner's Sources (a card counts for the owner of the account it settles to), and can be downloaded once nothing below blocks that report.
