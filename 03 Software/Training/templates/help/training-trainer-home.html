Training — Trainer area

This is the trainer's starting page in Training: the list of your students. From here you open a student to record their training needs, assign pills (short lessons about the menu's dishes) and follow their quiz results.

Typical flow

  1. Add the student, if they are not in the list yet.
  2. Open the student and add a training need (what they need to learn).
  3. Assign one or more pills to that need. The student then sees them on their own Training page.
  4. Come back to the student's page to follow the status of each pill and open each quiz attempt.

What this page shows

Trainer
Your name. Log out ends your session on this device.
Manage Training students
Opens RF-One's Students page: the Training students who sign in with an RF-One account, where you can add such a student and reset their password.
Students
Each current student with name, username and number of training needs recorded. Open opens the student's page.
Dismissed employees
Shown when some students are dismissed employees: their history stays readable through Open, but no new training can be assigned to them.

Buttons and what happens

Add student
Opens the form to create a student account of Training itself (username and password of Training). When you use Training from RF-One, a student signs in with an RF-One account instead: create them through Manage Training students.
