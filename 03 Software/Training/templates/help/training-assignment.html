This page is one pill assigned to you: the lesson about a dish, a practice check, and the graded final quiz. Opening it for the first time moves its status from Not started to In progress.

What you find here

Status and learning objectives
The pill's status (Not started, In progress, Completed) and what you are expected to learn.
The dish
Photo (when available), description, ingredients, preparation notes, how to sell it with example phrases, the current wine list pairings and why, and the allergens and dietary warnings to check with guests.
Practice check
Click an answer to see at once whether it is right and why. It is not a formal assessment and nothing is recorded. Try again clears it so you can start over.
Final quiz
Graded: 3 questions, 1 point each. Take the final quiz (or Take the final quiz again) opens it. Answers and explanations are shown only after you submit.
Your attempts
Every final-quiz attempt you made on this pill, with date and score; click a date to see that result again.

Next steps

Once you have taken the final quiz at least once the pill is Completed. Use the path at the top to go back to Training and open the next pill.
