How Training works

Training is your personal staff-training path. Your trainer records what you need to learn (your training needs) and assigns you pills: short lessons, each about one dish of the menu. You study each pill, check yourself, and take a short graded quiz. Completing a quiz records that you did the activity; it is not a certificate of competence.

How it works

  • Each pill shows the dish: description, ingredients, preparation notes, how to sell it, the wine pairings from the current wine list, and allergens and dietary warnings.
  • The Practice check inside a pill is for you only: it gives immediate feedback and is not recorded.
  • The Final quiz of a pill has 3 questions, 1 point each. Answers and explanations are shown only after you submit. You can retake it as often as you like; every attempt is kept and your trainer can see it.
  • When every pill assigned to you has been completed at least once, the Whole-path quiz becomes available: 3 questions for each of your pills, mixed together.

Typical flow

  1. On this page, find a pill under one of your training needs and press Open.
  2. Study the pill and try the Practice check.
  3. Take the pill's final quiz and read the result, with the right answers and their explanations.
  4. Repeat for every pill assigned to you.
  5. Take the whole-path quiz; its result lists the pills worth reviewing.

What this page shows

Welcome
Your name and Training username. Browse the dish guide opens the full guide of the menu's dishes. Log out ends your session on this device.
Whole-path quiz
Whether the whole-path quiz is available; when it is, Take the whole-path quiz starts it. Your previous attempts are listed with date and score; click a date to see that result again.
Your training needs
One box per need recorded by your trainer (with its origin, when given), listing the pills assigned to it. Status: Not started (never opened), In progress (opened, final quiz not yet taken) or Completed (final quiz taken at least once, whatever the score). Last score is your latest final-quiz result.

If something is missing

If no need or no pill appears, nothing has been assigned to you yet: ask your trainer.
