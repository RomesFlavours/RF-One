Whole-path quiz

This quiz covers your whole path at once: 3 questions for each distinct pill assigned to you, 1 point each, in mixed order. It is available once every pill assigned to you has been completed at least once.

What you can do

  • Choose one answer for each question.
  • Submit to see your score, the right answers, their explanations and the pills worth reviewing.

Buttons and what happens

Submit whole-path quiz
Records this attempt with its score and opens the result. If some questions are unanswered, you are asked to confirm first; an unanswered question scores zero. A submitted attempt cannot be changed or deleted, but you can take the quiz again from the Training page: every attempt is kept and visible to your trainer. Pressing the button twice records only one attempt.

Next steps

To leave without submitting, use the path at the top: nothing is recorded.
