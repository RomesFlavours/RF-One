This is the graded quiz of one pill: 3 questions, 1 point each. Your answers are recorded when you submit.

What you can do

  • Choose one answer for each question.
  • Submit the quiz to see your score, the right answers and their explanations.

Buttons and what happens

Submit final quiz
Records this attempt with its score and opens the result. If some questions are unanswered, you are asked to confirm first; an unanswered question scores zero. A submitted attempt cannot be changed or deleted, but you can take the quiz again as often as you like: every attempt is kept and visible to your trainer. Pressing the button twice records only one attempt.

Next steps

Submitting marks the pill as Completed. To leave without submitting, use the path at the top: nothing is recorded.
