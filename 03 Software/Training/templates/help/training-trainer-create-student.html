Add student

This form creates a new student account of Training itself, with its own username and password, so that you can record the student's training needs and assign pills to them.

When Training is opened from RF-One, people sign in with their RF-One account, not with a Training username: a student created here cannot sign in there until an RF-One administrator links them to an RF-One account. To add a student who signs in with RF-One, use Manage Training students in the trainer area.

Fields

Display name
The student's name as it will appear in Training.
Username
The name the student signs in to Training with. It must not already be in use.
Initial password
The student's first password, shown as you type it. Give it to the student directly (in person or by voice): RF-One does not send it by email or SMS.

Buttons and what happens

Create student
Creates the account and opens the new student's page. If the username is already taken or a field is not accepted, the form stays open with the reason and nothing is created. A student account cannot be deleted from Training; the password can be changed later on the student's page.

Next steps

On the student's page, add a training need and assign pills to it.
