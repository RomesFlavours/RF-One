## What changed

1. **AI timeout and web workers.** One interview turn waits at most 45 s for the AI (`interviews/provider.py`, `AI_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`), so the whole request stays under CloudFront's 60 s `OriginReadTimeout` for rfone-web. `ai_client.generate_json` takes an optional keyword `timeout`: with it, one attempt (Bedrock `read_timeout`, `connect_timeout=10`, `max_attempts=1`; Anthropic `timeout`, `max_retries=0`). On expiry the participant sees "The AI took too long to answer. Please try again" (HTTP 503) and the saved conversation is unchanged. Callers that pass no timeout (Selection, Bank AI rules, Tips) are now bounded too: the read timeout is `RFONE_AI_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` when set, else 100 s, with at most 2 attempts (Bedrock standard retry mode; Anthropic `max_retries=1`). The rfone-web Dockerfile sets **`ENV RFONE_AI_TIMEOUT_SECONDS=45`**, so on rfone-web such a call holds a thread for at most about 90 s (2 attempts of 45 s), never minutes. Bedrock clients are created once per configuration under a lock and reused (boto3 client creation is not thread-safe). The Dockerfile runs `gunicorn --workers 2 --threads 4 --worker-class gthread --timeout 120`: 4 threads (not 8) because a request can hold two database connections, so one worker needs at most 8 of SQLAlchemy's default pool of 15. In gthread mode `--timeout` does not bound a request; the AI limits do. `database.py` adds `pool_pre_ping=True` and `pool_timeout=10` for server databases (not SQLite), default pool sizes. `interviews.js` shows the "try again" text when a gateway answers 502/503/504 with an HTML page, and "Interviews are not available yet" when RF-One's own 503 page says so.
2. **AI answer ceiling.** `ai_client.MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS = 16000` replaces 4,096 in both provider calls. The change is byte-identical to `3a08239` on `shelbi/selection-position-profiles`, so the two branches merge without a conflict.
3. **Consent.** The Start page has a required "I agree" checkbox: answers are saved in RF-One and used to build the company's know-how, and administrators can delete them. The server refuses "start" without it (HTTP 400, no AI call). `interview_sessions.consent_given` and `consent_at` record it.
   **Interviews that existed before the migration** read as "no consent recorded". When the participant next answers or completes one, the same "I agree" box is required and checked on the server before any AI call; the first accepted turn records the consent. Until then its answers and results are visible only to the account that requested it (not to other Interviews admins), and its results page and the list show "Consent not recorded".
4. **Delete and seeing every interview: Domain administrators of ORGANIZATION only.** An account that is a Domain administrator of ORGANIZATION (`account_is_domain_admin`: RF-One administrator, or the Domain administrator role on ORGANIZATION) sees every interview and can delete one with its answers and results (CSRF-protected POST, required confirmation box checked again on the server). Every other account with the Interviews item sees and manages only the interviews it requested and has no Delete button (a forged delete gets 403). Each deletion adds an `INTERVIEW_DELETE` event to the person's directory history (who, when, interview id, status; no content).
5. **Quotations.** Quotations are compared with the answers after normalising case, quote marks, apostrophes, dashes, the ellipsis and whitespace. A supporting quotation must be at least 3 words or 15 characters. A candidate whose quotation is too short or still not found is left out instead of failing the whole Complete; the results page shows how many were left out. The stored and displayed quotation is the matching passage of the participant's answer, as they wrote it, not the AI's copy.
6. **Results visibility.** See 3 and 4. The list has a "Requested by" column.
7. **Error handling.** On the participant page, any failed turn (AI error, invalid input, conflict) now checks that the interview still exists after the rollback, and answers 404 if an administrator deleted it meanwhile, instead of reading a deleted row.
