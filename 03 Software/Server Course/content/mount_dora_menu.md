# Mount Dora menu

Received 27 October 2026 as a designed two-page menu. No prices yet. No dish cards yet. A star (*) marks a Rome's Flavours signature dish.

## Antipasti

- **House Focaccia:** warm focaccia, extra-virgin olive oil, sea salt
- **Prosciutto e Melone:** Parma prosciutto, ripe melon, aged balsamic
- **Polpette al Sugo:** house-made beef and pork meatballs, tomato sauce, Parmigiano, grilled bread
- **Arancini:** crisp risotto croquettes, mozzarella, basil tomato sauce
- **Calamari alla Griglia:** grilled calamari, lemon, garlic, parsley, chili
- **Antipasto Italiano:** selection of Italian cured meats, cheeses, olives, roasted vegetables, focaccia
- **Bruschetta Trio:** tomato and basil; whipped ricotta and roasted peppers; goat cheese and caramelized onion

## Zuppe e Insalate

- **Crema di Pomodoro:** creamy tomato and basil soup, served with bread
- **Zuppa del Giorno:** chef's daily seasonal soup
- **Insalata Verde:** mixed greens, cucumber, fennel, lemon vinaigrette
- **Vegetable Garden Salad:** mixed greens, tomatoes, olives, roasted peppers, artichokes, red onion, red wine vinaigrette
- **Burrata Salad:** burrata, arugula, tomatoes, basil, olive oil
- **Add to any salad:** grilled chicken, salmon, shrimp, buffalo mozzarella

## Pasta Fresca

All pasta is made fresh in-house daily.

- **Tagliolini alla Carbonara*:** egg yolk, guanciale, Pecorino Romano, black pepper
- **Fettuccine alla Bolognese*:** slow-cooked beef and pork ragu
- **Tagliolini Cacio e Pepe:** Pecorino Romano, black pepper
- **Fettuccine Gamberi e Pistacchio*:** shrimp, pistachio, garlic, parsley, white wine, olive oil
- **Gnocchi alla Sorrentina:** house-made gnocchi, tomato sauce, melted mozzarella, basil
- **Gnocchi al Pesto:** house-made gnocchi, basil pesto, Parmigiano
- **Tagliolini al Pomodoro:** Italian plum tomato sauce, garlic, basil, olive oil
- **Linguine alle Vongole:** clams, garlic, white wine, parsley, chili
- **Fettuccine Alfredo:** Parmigiano cream sauce
- **Printed under the pasta:** add mushrooms, add bacon, add truffle (which pastas these go with is still to be confirmed)

## Pizza Gourmet

Naturally leavened dough with imported Italian flour.

- **Via Napoli (Margherita):** tomato, mozzarella, basil
- **Mercati Traianei:** Amatriciana sauce, mozzarella, red onion, guanciale, Grana Padano
- **Testaccio:** tomato, mozzarella, Calabrian chili, spicy oil
- **Piazza di Spagna:** tomato, mozzarella, red peppers, black olives
- **Trinita dei Monti:** tomato, mozzarella, zucchini, eggplant, red peppers, basil
- **Rome's Flavours*:** tomato, mozzarella, Italian sausage, broccoli, Grana Padano
- **Sicilia:** tomato, mozzarella, eggplant, ricotta salata
- **Villa Borghese:** tomato, mozzarella, sausage, mushrooms, white truffle oil
- **Colosseo:** tomato, mozzarella, spicy salami, gorgonzola, red peppers
- **Prosciutto e Rucola:** tomato, mozzarella, Parma prosciutto, arugula, shaved Parmigiano
- **White Dolomiti:** mozzarella, porcini mushrooms, speck, white truffle oil

## Secondi

- **Branzino al Forno*:** whole roasted branzino, lemon, garlic, fresh herbs
- **Salmone Arrosto:** roasted salmon, seasonal vegetables, lemon herb sauce
- **Pollo alla Puttanesca:** chicken breast with taggiasche olives, capers, cherry tomatoes
- **Tagliata di Manzo:** sliced grilled steak, arugula, cherry tomatoes, Parmigiano, balsamic
- **Brasato di Manzo:** red wine braised short rib, creamy polenta
- **Costolette d'Agnello alle Erbe:** herb-seared lamb chops, roasted potatoes
- **Melanzane alla Parmigiana:** baked eggplant, tomato sauce, mozzarella, basil
