One engine, two directions

In Selection the AI takes information in: it reads the CVs and interviews the candidates. In Training it gives information back: it teaches the Server Course, one unit at a time. People decide.

Step 1 · CVs in order

Candidates

Upload the CVs from Indeed or direct applications. The AI reads each one and puts them in order by chance of being hired, with the red flags to check. Call the first ones on the list.

Step 2 · before the in-person interview

The interview link

After your call, send the candidate their link from their page. On their own phone, the AI checks availability, the questions their CV raises, and real situations on a shift. You read the answers, score them, and invite them in.
