# Automatic import reconciliation and reusable transaction photos

Approved 2026-10-19: every committed new bank movement runs general extraction using description, memo and Source, then recognition photos, then sourced WHO research when no complete photo resolves it. Deterministic validated criteria execute without repeated AI calls. AI fills an extraction gap from literal bank evidence, rechecks photos, and researches public WHO identity only after no photo resolves the movement. Website findings are proposals, not automatic payment-purpose decisions.

Import staging previews roll back everything including queued jobs. Only committed jobs are processed, outside the import write transaction. Jobs carry transaction-state fingerprints and worker leases: provider calls do not hold database write locks; later manual edits cause stale jobs to stop, never overwrite. Failed provider calls are explicit and retryable, not fabricated output. No retroactive queue is created by migration.

Each row offers Save as photo. Capture reads current WHO/WHY/WHOM and displays editable descriptor matching plus fixed Source and direction. Missing fields and split/uncertain allocations are refused with instructions. The person reviews the exact criterion and chooses future-only or archive+future. Existing mandatory archive preview and guarded rollback are reused. Origin classification and catalog versions are bound to the signed confirmation. An existing exact photo is updated rather than duplicated. Original bank facts and economic allocations are protected by canonical services.

Implementation notes: WHO-identity, literal/exact-description and optional memo criteria are supported, with exact Source ID and direction. A capture includes the current accounting destination as an explicit validated assignment, preserving a human-approved exception if the WHY master mapping later changes. Proven narrower photo conditions supersede a broader compatible signature; incomparable conflicting photos require clarification. All participating photos retain before/after snapshots for import rollback.

Jobs freeze the rule-version identities enabled at their import. Future-only changes do not retroactively affect previously queued movements. New jobs are sealed after bank normalization, duplicate decisions and transfer matching; multi-file imports refresh guards before commit. The worker reuses recent sourced WHO knowledge, and unresolved/provider-failed/stale results are visible per row. Retry requires CSRF and refuses records modified after import. Neutral live checks verified both the configured ChatGPT connection and actual web search without using financial data.
