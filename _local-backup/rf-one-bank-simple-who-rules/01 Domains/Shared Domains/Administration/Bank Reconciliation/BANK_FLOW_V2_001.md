# Bank: validated format discovery, identity extraction and counterparty photos

The user-approved flow processes one canonical movement at a time. Active extraction rules read standard descriptions and return a WHO in memory, without writing classifications. A matching complete photo supplies WHY and WHOM and marks the movement Reconciled. Missing extraction or missing photo proceeds to ChatGPT comparison with eligible photos. Only a unique certain match with confidence at least 0.95 can proceed to the photo decision. Otherwise public web research produces a probable-WHO proposal for human review, without writing WHO/WHY/WHOM.

The page has two groups: Regole AI and Foto segnaletiche. Prior counterparty knowledge, sources and saved recognition instructions are presented together. Prior rules and immutable versions remain available, with extraction ignoring any former purpose assignment. Incomplete legacy photos are identified and cannot invent a WHOM. Rename/deletion retain the existing audit and rollback safeguards; deleting a whole WHO photo removes its linked cases from execution.

Cerca nuovi formati scans every stored movement, groups recurring descriptions and asks ChatGPT about representative examples from all uncovered groups. Repeated identical descriptions are included. Proposals are durable and require individual human review on actual records. Existing extractor variants are proposed as edits rather than duplicate rules. No discovery automatically creates an operative rule or changes a movement.

Conversations, proposals and exact previews are stored in bank_flow_artifacts and bound to the authenticated account. ChatGPT proposes a restricted extraction/photo specification, never executable code or SQL. Photos define allowed WHY/WHOM sets, conditional branches, exceptions and any required information providers. New catalog entries require explicit human creation. Necessary internal/external retrieval methods must be clarified and connected before a dependent photo becomes operative; provider results must be verified. No Amazon API or universal external connection is assumed. Built-in read-only sources provide the RF-One WHO catalog/history and public web facts; public research receives no bank description, reference, account or amount. Private authenticated sources require their own connection. Trusted adapters register in app.extensions['bank_photo_evidence']; arbitrary model-suggested URLs/code are never executed. Each adapter receives the human-approved retrieval instructions and movement facts, and returns verified facts and provenance.

Every new or modified policy requires an actual-record preview before saving. The human chooses future only, or archive and future. Previews expire after 30 minutes and bind the draft, previous rule version, catalogs, source/ownership configuration, match exclusions and all eligible movement snapshots. Changes invalidate the preview. Archive applies only complete decisions; unresolved matching rows are queued for guarded human-review proposals. No old transaction is reprocessed simply by deployment or a future-only save. Previously queued jobs retain their eligible version snapshots.

Writes use canonical decisions and balanced allocations; applications retain exact before/after snapshots. Archive operations group writes by photo, so one rollback covers all rows in that application. Later changes to any movement, decision, allocation or source cause rollback to skip the affected row. Bank source fields are never restored or replaced. Validated complete automatic photos have a distinct Reconciled status; accounting export still blocks a photo whose WHY lacks an accounting destination. Historical automatic suggestions never become confirmed merely by deployment.

Verification: 18 synthetic flow checks cover extraction, actual CSV import idempotence, conditional photos, trusted retrieval, AI matching, uncertainty, web-only proposals, concurrent manual changes, human validation, future/archive scopes, stale previews, discovery, explicit catalog creation and grouped rollback. Eight canonical application safety tests and five rule management tests pass. An isolated copy preview scanned all 3,299 eligible movements; the Zelle extractor matched 679 without changing them. A real ChatGPT call with synthetic examples verified the new structured extraction response. Deployment backs up both the database and modified source, adds only the artifact table, and verifies every pre-existing stored column remains unchanged.
