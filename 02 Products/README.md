# RF-One Products

## Purpose

`02 Products/` holds canonical Product-level documentation: commercial applications built by combining Core capabilities and one or more Domains, designed to create measurable value for their users.

---

# Authority

Products are **canonical for their own commercial configuration** — which Domains and Core capabilities they combine, and how — but they do not redefine Core or Domain semantics.

A Product must answer: who pays, why they pay, and what measurable economic value RF-One creates for them (see `CLAUDE.md`, Product Philosophy).

---

# What belongs here

- Definition of a specific commercial Product: which Domains it combines, its scope, its target user, its value proposition.
- Product-specific configuration choices built on canonical Core/Domain concepts.

# What does not belong here

- Universal Core ontology — `00 Core/`.
- Reusable Domain knowledge — `01 Domains/`.
- RF-One's own company-level commercial strategy, pricing philosophy, or business model — `09 Strategy/`.
- Runtime implementation — `03 Software/`.

---

# Current status

No canonical Product documentation exists yet. `03 Software/InvoiceIntake/` is current software/prototype tooling that a future Product may use, but Software and Product are distinct layers — see `03 Software/README.md`.
