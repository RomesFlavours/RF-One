# RF-One Research

## Purpose

`05 Research/` holds research, exploration, competitor studies, experiments, external technical investigation, and other non-canonical analytical material that may inform RF-One's direction.

---

# Authority

Research is **not canonical**. It may influence architecture, Domain design or Strategy, but a finding here becomes authoritative only after it is formalized in the appropriate canonical location (`00 Core/`, `01 Domains/`, `02 Products/`, or `09 Strategy/`).

---

# What belongs here

- Market research, competitor analysis, technical investigation, prototypes-as-research, exploratory notes.
- Methodology material, such as a future `Methods/` subdirectory for knowledge-acquisition approaches (e.g. structured interviews).

# What does not belong here

- Approved architecture — even if it originated from research here, it must be formally incorporated into Core/Domain/Product/Strategy documentation to become authoritative.
- Meeting notes — `06 Meetings/`.
