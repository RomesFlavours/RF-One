# RF-One Strategy

## Purpose

`09 Strategy/` is the canonical location for RF-One's own strategy as a company/product business — distinct from the business Domains RF-One models for its customers (`01 Domains/`), and distinct from universal Core ontology (`00 Core/`).

---

# Authority

This layer is **canonical for RF-One's company-level strategy** once populated and approved. It must never be confused with Core ontology: a commercial objective (e.g. "maximize economic profit") is a strategic choice of RF-One as a business, not a universal claim about what every Subject modeled by RF-One must want.

---

# What belongs here (future)

This area is intended for future documentation such as:

- commercial strategy;
- business model;
- value measurement (including counterfactual value-generated measurement);
- service/delivery strategy (e.g. SaaS vs. licensed software rationale);
- product portfolio strategy;
- company-level knowledge governance (e.g. how RF-One's own Core Domain evolves across customers/tenants);
- multi-tenant/shared-intelligence strategy.

# What does not belong here

- Universal Core ontology — `00 Core/`.
- Customer business Domain knowledge — `01 Domains/`.
- Product-level configuration — `02 Products/`.
