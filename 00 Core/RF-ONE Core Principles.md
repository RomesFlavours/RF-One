# Purpose

This document defines the immutable principles governing the RF-ONE Core.

Every RF-ONE concept belongs to one and only one layer:

- Core
- Domains
- Environment

The Core models universal reality.
Domains model business knowledge.
Environment models contextual knowledge.

## Principles

1. Core before technology.
2. Only universal concepts belong to the Core.
3. Reality before software.
4. Every concept with identity is an Entity.
5. Identity is immutable.
6. Relationships are independent.
7. Goals define outcomes only.
8. Processes define how goals are achieved.
9. The system chooses the executor according to available capabilities and resources.
10. Business knowledge is expressed through Processes.
11. Composition before specialization.
12. Single Source of Truth.
13. Extensibility without breaking existing concepts.
14. AI reasons about the Core but is not part of it.
15. Facts are persisted; conclusions are inferred whenever possible.
16. RF-One models a Subject in relation to Reality; Reality is never assumed complete, and the Subject is never assumed rational.
17. A Desire is sovereign: it need not be rational, feasible or currently actionable to remain valid. A Goal is a clarified, confirmed Desire pursuable within understood Reality; it does not require a Process to already exist.
18. Decision is a first-class Core concept. Core ontology is independent from Runtime persistence: a Decision is not automatically an Entity, and is not automatically persisted.
19. RF-One may act as a Business Autopilot, deciding and acting within explicitly Delegated Authority, while the Subject retains ultimate sovereignty over direction, Goals, constraints and override. Human control does not imply continuous human operation.
20. RF-One should reason about a Subject's Net/Retained Outcome after External Obligations/Claims, not merely a nominal Gross Outcome, and may recognize that some Constraints are not immutable, surfacing lawful Constraint Shaping and Counterfactual Structural Comparison. Lawful optimization is never equivalent to evasion, fraud, misrepresentation, concealment, false reporting or sham transactions.
21. Every meaningful Decision and Action must be attributable to an identifiable Acting Identity, performed within demonstrable Authority (direct, role-derived, or explicitly Delegated), and reconstructable afterward through Auditability. No Domain or Module may define its own independent identity, authority or audit mechanism; these are shared RF-One infrastructure.
22. RF-One must itself embody managerial intelligence, understanding the Subject and Reality closely enough to identify what requires attention even when the Subject does not know what to ask, adapting its intellectual role to the Subject's contextual capability rather than mirroring the Subject's reasoning, while understanding the Subject never transfers the Subject's authority to RF-One.
23. RF-One must execute its Processes autonomously within established rules and Delegated Authority, verify their result against the expected Outcome rather than presenting an unverified result as achieved, and involve the competent Acting Identity only when a genuine human contribution is required — never as a routine substitute for already-authorized autonomous execution merely relocated onto a different interaction channel.
24. A Process's execution may progress chronologically through Planning, Scheduling/Programming, Management and Operations; a Process need not formally contain all four, none of them is defined by who or what performs it, and Reporting, Administration, Analysis and Feedback are not additional phases of this sequence.
25. Process ownership belongs to a Position, not directly to the person who occupies it: every Process, and every phase of a Process, must be attributable to at least one responsible Position, whose scope is expressed through the existing Corporate/Brand/Operational Unit/Operational Area/Domain/Module hierarchy, so that autonomous execution never removes the organization's ability to attribute an exception, an out-of-delegation decision, or an unresolved problem to a concrete responsibility.
26. RF-One must determine, from available and relevant context, whether a matter genuinely requires human attention, who should receive it, with what priority, through what channel and at what level of synthesis; it may decide how and when to surface a matter, but must never decide to ignore one, and Critical matters must always be actively surfaced and persistently recorded, never silenced or aggregated away.
27. RF-One may recognize explicit and implicit triggers from the canonical knowledge of a Process and of Reality, without depending on a centralized hardcoded trigger list; trigger discovery never creates Business Rules, extends Authority, or by itself authorizes any consequence, and every consequence of a recognized trigger remains bound by the Process, its Rules, and Delegated Authority.
28. Cognito is RF-One's Cognitive Intelligence. Human Interaction and Trigger Intelligence are capabilities of Cognito; they may be technically separated, but they consume the same canonical Business Knowledge and remain subordinate to Process, Rules and Authority.

# Guiding Principle

The Core is the permanent intellectual asset of RF-ONE.
Technologies, Domains and AI evolve.
The Core remains stable because it models reality itself.
