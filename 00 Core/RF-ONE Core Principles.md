# Purpose

This document defines the immutable principles governing the RF-ONE Core.

Every RF-ONE concept belongs to one and only one layer:

- Core
- Domains
- Environment

The Core models universal reality.
Domains model business knowledge.
Environment models contextual knowledge.

## Principles

1. Core before technology.
2. Only universal concepts belong to the Core.
3. Reality before software.
4. Every concept with identity is an Entity.
5. Identity is immutable.
6. Relationships are independent.
7. Goals define outcomes only.
8. Processes define how goals are achieved.
9. The system chooses the executor according to available capabilities and resources.
10. Business knowledge is expressed through Processes.
11. Composition before specialization.
12. Single Source of Truth.
13. Extensibility without breaking existing concepts.
14. AI reasons about the Core but is not part of it.
15. Facts are persisted; conclusions are inferred whenever possible.
16. RF-One models a Subject in relation to Reality; Reality is never assumed complete, and the Subject is never assumed rational.
17. A Desire is sovereign: it need not be rational, feasible or currently actionable to remain valid. A Goal is a clarified, confirmed Desire pursuable within understood Reality; it does not require a Process to already exist.
18. Decision is a first-class Core concept. Core ontology is independent from Runtime persistence: a Decision is not automatically an Entity, and is not automatically persisted.
19. RF-One may act as a Business Autopilot, deciding and acting within explicitly Delegated Authority, while the Subject retains ultimate sovereignty over direction, Goals, constraints and override. Human control does not imply continuous human operation.
20. RF-One should reason about a Subject's Net/Retained Outcome after External Obligations/Claims, not merely a nominal Gross Outcome, and may recognize that some Constraints are not immutable, surfacing lawful Constraint Shaping and Counterfactual Structural Comparison. Lawful optimization is never equivalent to evasion, fraud, misrepresentation, concealment, false reporting or sham transactions.

# Guiding Principle

The Core is the permanent intellectual asset of RF-ONE.
Technologies, Domains and AI evolve.
The Core remains stable because it models reality itself.
