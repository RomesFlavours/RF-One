## Purpose

This document extends the Core `Outcome` concept (see [03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md](03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md)) with two related reasoning capabilities:

1. distinguishing a **Gross Outcome** from the **Net / Retained Outcome** the Subject actually keeps after **External Obligations / Claims**;
2. recognizing that some Constraints are not immutable, and that a Subject may lawfully pursue **Constraint Shaping** — Decisions/Actions that change its *future* relationship to Reality (future constraints, obligations, authority, risks, opportunities) — and comparing such alternative structures counterfactually.

It also establishes the boundary between lawful optimization and unlawful or deceptive behavior, and clarifies how both capabilities integrate with the Epistemic Boundary and Temporal Coherence.

**Taxation is one important application of this capability, not its definition.** Core supplies general reasoning semantics here; it does not encode tax law. See Section 11.

## 1. Gross Outcome vs Net / Retained Outcome

An `Outcome` (see [03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md](03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md)) may have layers:

```text
Gross Outcome
- External Obligations / Claims
- Required Costs / Transfers
= Net / Retained Outcome
```

- **Gross Outcome** — the Outcome as directly produced by the Action, before external claims are applied.
- **Net / Retained Outcome** — what the Subject actually retains once relevant External Obligations / Claims (Section 2) have been accounted for.

> RF-One should reason about the Subject's real retained Outcome after relevant obligations and unavoidable external claims, not merely the nominal result.

Net / Retained Outcome is a further distinction *within* the existing `Outcome` concept, not a replacement for it, and not a new stage of the operational cycle.

Do not require every Outcome to be monetary. In business/economic contexts this pattern commonly appears as:

```text
Gross Economic Value
→ taxes / fees / interest / compliance cost / transaction cost
→ Retained Economic Value
```

but the same gross-minus-claims pattern may apply to non-monetary Outcomes wherever Reality imposes a claim on what was produced (for example, time owed, capacity committed, or reputation conditioned by a disclosure obligation). Do not create a universal financial utility function: Net / Retained Outcome is a lens a Domain may apply when relevant, not a mandatory scalar every Goal must produce.

**Net / Retained Outcome does not imply every Goal is economic.** A Desire or Goal (see [02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md](02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md)) remains valid regardless of whether it has an economic dimension; this distinction applies only where the Outcome in question is, in fact, subject to External Obligations / Claims.

## 2. External Obligations / Claims

Reality (see [01_Subject_and_Reality.md](01_Subject_and_Reality.md)) may impose obligations that reduce, redirect, condition, delay, or otherwise alter the value a Subject retains from a Gross Outcome.

Examples may include:

```text
taxes
regulatory obligations
contractual payments
fees
debt service
insurance requirements
transaction costs
licensing costs
mandatory transfers
```

This is illustrative, not an exhaustive or mandatory taxonomy. **Core does not encode a fixed taxonomy of obligations.** Concrete obligation types, rates and rules are supplied by a Domain (for example a Taxation Domain) and/or by Runtime knowledge sources — see Section 11.

The Core-level point is structural: such claims are part of Reality, and Reality Check (see [02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md](02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md)) must include them when clarifying expected Outcomes, rather than evaluating only the Gross Outcome.

## 3. Constraint Shaping

Reality includes Constraints (see [01_Subject_and_Reality.md](01_Subject_and_Reality.md)). **Not every Constraint is immutable.**

**Constraint Shaping** — a Subject may lawfully make Decisions and take Actions that change its *future* relationship to Reality, so that different future constraints, obligations, authority, risks, opportunities, or economic consequences apply.

Examples may include:

```text
changing organizational structure
changing contractual structure
changing ownership
changing jurisdiction
changing financing
changing transaction timing
```

This is illustrative, not exhaustive. Constraint Shaping is enacted through the ordinary Decision → Action cycle (see Section 8); it is not a separate mechanism outside it.

Constraint Shaping is distinct from violating, concealing, or ignoring a Constraint — see Section 5 for the boundary.

**Do not assume every Constraint is mutable.** Most of Reality remains a genuine constraint on the Subject. Constraint Shaping applies only where Reality (law, contract, physics, or another genuine limit) actually allows a lawful alternative structure — it is a capability to recognize and evaluate such openings where they exist, not a presumption that they always exist.

## 4. Counterfactual Structural Comparison

RF-One should be able to compare:

```text
Current Structure
vs
Alternative Structure A
vs
Alternative Structure B
```

using expected Outcomes. Where relevant, comparison may include:

```text
gross outcome
net / retained outcome
implementation cost
transition cost
ongoing administrative cost
risk
uncertainty
reversibility
option value
time horizon
future flexibility
compliance burden
```

**Do not require a universal scalar score.** These dimensions are surfaced to the Subject as tradeoffs (see Section 9, Subject Sovereignty), not collapsed into a single automatic ranking.

Counterfactual Structural Comparison may occur prospectively, before a structural Decision is made, or retrospectively as Learning (see [03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md](03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md)) about how an alternative structure would have performed.

## 5. Legality / Compliance / Truthfulness Boundary

Core must distinguish **lawful optimization** from:

```text
evasion
fraud
misrepresentation
concealment
false reporting
sham transactions
```

This is not a moralizing layer. It is part of Reality and constraint reasoning: deception about a structure does not actually change the Subject's relationship to Reality — it misstates it, which corrupts the Epistemic Boundary (Section 7) and the Subject's own model of Reality.

**The operational distinction Core requires:**

- **Constraint Shaping** changes the Subject's actual relationship to Reality — a real, verifiable structure that genuinely applies going forward.
- **Evasion, fraud, misrepresentation, concealment, false reporting and sham transactions** instead misstate or conceal the Subject's actual relationship to Reality while pretending it is something else, or pretending a change has occurred when it has not.

RF-One may optimize within lawful available choices, but must not treat deceptive or unlawful behavior as an ordinary equivalent strategy. In Counterfactual Structural Comparison (Section 4), an unlawful or deceptive "structure" is not an admissible alternative to weigh against lawful structures as if it were a comparable risk-adjusted option.

## 6. Jurisdiction and Temporal Context

Rules governing External Obligations / Claims and the availability of a given structural alternative are often dependent on:

```text
jurisdiction
effective date
status
structure
transaction sequence
elections/choices
facts and circumstances
```

Consistent with Temporal Coherence (see [04_Temporal_Coherence_and_Evolution.md](04_Temporal_Coherence_and_Evolution.md)), such rules are temporally and contextually situated. **A rule confirmed true in one jurisdiction, date, or set of facts must never be silently generalized as timeless or universal.**

## 7. Epistemic Requirements

Obligation and structural reasoning reuses the existing Core Epistemic Boundary (see [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md)): Fact, Observation, Evidence, Belief, Assumption, Inference, Hypothesis, Unknown. **This document does not create a parallel epistemic system.**

For high-impact obligation/structure reasoning, preserve — where relevant — alongside any conclusion:

```text
source
authority
jurisdiction
effective date
assumptions
interpretation
uncertainty
unknowns
```

**A legal/tax interpretation must never be silently promoted to Fact.** An interpretation of how a rule applies to a Subject's facts and circumstances is ordinarily a Belief, Inference or Hypothesis until confirmed by a competent authority or otherwise established — see [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md), Section 1.

## 8. Integration with the Core Loop

This capability integrates into the existing Subject ↔ Reality loop rather than introducing a separate cycle:

```text
Subject ↔ Reality
        ↓
Desire / Goal
        ↓
Reality Check
        ↓
Alternative Structures / Actions
        ↓
Decision
        ↓
Action
        ↓
Gross Outcome
        ↓
Obligations / Claims / Required Costs
        ↓
Net / Retained Outcome
        ↓
Learning
```

Structural choices (Section 3) are themselves Decisions and Actions in the sense already defined by [03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md](03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md), and — like any Action — may change future Reality (see [01_Subject_and_Reality.md](01_Subject_and_Reality.md)).

## 9. Subject Sovereignty

Subject Sovereignty (see [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md)) applies unchanged. RF-One may surface:

```text
alternative structures
after-obligation consequences
risk
uncertainty
compliance burden
tradeoffs
```

but the Subject remains final strategic authority within lawful and delegated boundaries (see [06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md](06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md) for Delegated Authority).

## 10. Business Relevance (illustrative, not Core-defining)

Core should support business reasoning such as:

```text
operating profit
vs
after-tax retained value
```

or:

```text
higher nominal return
vs
lower-risk after-obligation return
```

without hard-coding business concepts into Core. This remains compatible with the commercial direction of a Business Autopilot under human command (see [06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md](06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md)) and with maximizing real economic value rather than merely top-line output — but these are illustrative applications of Sections 1–5, not Core requirements.

## 11. Boundary with Taxation (and other Obligation Domains)

Core does **not** define:

```text
tax rates
deductions
credits
depreciation rules
entity-specific tax rules
filing obligations
tax forms
tax jurisdictions as a fixed taxonomy
```

These belong to a transversal Taxation Domain and/or Runtime knowledge sources (see [../../01 Domains/README.md](../../01%20Domains/README.md)).

**Core supplies reasoning semantics** — Gross/Net Outcome, External Obligation/Claim, Constraint Shaping, the lawful-optimization boundary, and the epistemic handling of obligation/structure conclusions. **A Taxation Domain (or any other Domain with external claims on Outcome — regulatory compliance, contractual obligation management, and similar) supplies the concrete obligation knowledge**, applying these Core concepts without redefining them.

---

## 12. Consistency Notes

- **Net / Retained Outcome does not imply every Goal is economic.** It is an Outcome-layer distinction, applicable only when the specific Outcome in question is, in fact, subject to External Obligations / Claims. Desire and Goal (see [02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md](02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md)) remain general and are not redefined in economic terms.
- **Constraint Shaping does not imply every constraint can be changed.** Most of Reality remains a genuine, immutable constraint. The capability applies only where Reality genuinely permits a lawful alternative structure, and recognizing that a constraint is *not* shapeable is as valid an outcome of this reasoning as recognizing that it is.
