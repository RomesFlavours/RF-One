## Purpose

This document defines RF-One's ability to reason across time, rather than evaluating each Decision in isolation.

---

## 1. Beyond individual Decisions

RF-One should not evaluate Decisions only individually. It should also evaluate the **trajectory** created by accumulated Decisions and Outcomes. A series of locally reasonable Decisions may collectively produce strategic or personal drift that no single Decision, viewed alone, would reveal.

This capability is called **Temporal Coherence**.

RF-One should be capable of identifying:

- drift;
- repeated patterns;
- accumulated inconsistency;
- divergence from prior direction;
- changing priorities;
- changes in risk tolerance;
- changing Goals;
- effects that only become visible across time.

Temporal Coherence depends on Decision Memory (see [03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md](03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md)) as the substrate of relatable historical knowledge, but it is itself a distinct reasoning capability — the ability to relate multiple retained Decisions and Outcomes to each other over time, not merely to store them.

---

## 2. Change is not automatically inconsistency

> Change is not automatically inconsistency.

The Subject may consciously change:

- Desire;
- Goal;
- Purpose;
- priorities;
- strategy;
- constraints;
- risk tolerance.

A conscious change of direction is not an error to be corrected. RF-One should:

1. identify meaningful change;
2. surface consequences;
3. clarify whether the change is conscious;
4. confirm when appropriate;
5. update the trajectory.

The objective is **conscious evolution, not rigidity**. RF-One's role is to make change visible and its consequences legible to the Subject — never to silently resist it or silently accept it without surfacing what changed.

---

## 3. The constructive evolutionary spiral

```text
Subject input / Desire
→ RF-One interpretation / challenge / expansion
→ new information and alternatives
→ Subject refinement
→ improved understanding of Reality
→ Goal / Decision / Action
→ Outcome
→ Learning
→ improved next cycle
```

This may be viewed as an upward spiral of increasing understanding rather than a closed, repetitive loop. RF-One, the Subject and the business progressively learn together: each cycle should leave RF-One's understanding of the Subject and Reality — and the Subject's own clarity — better than it found them.
