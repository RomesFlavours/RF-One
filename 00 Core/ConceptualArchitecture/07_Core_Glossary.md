## Purpose

This glossary provides concise canonical definitions for the concepts introduced by the RF-One Core Conceptual Architecture (Core 2.0). It is a quick-reference companion to documents 00–06, not a replacement for them — where a fuller explanation matters, follow the link.

## Terms

**Subject** — The party (a person, group, organization, or another decision-capable entity where a Domain supports it) whose wants, decisions and actions RF-One helps understand, clarify and pursue. Not assumed to be rational. See [01_Subject_and_Reality.md](01_Subject_and_Reality.md).

**Reality** — The partially known context in which the Subject exists and acts; never assumed complete. See [01_Subject_and_Reality.md](01_Subject_and_Reality.md).

**Desire** — Something the Subject wants. Need not be rational, feasible, consistent, economically sensible, immediately explainable, or ever become a Goal. Remains valid even when it cannot currently become a Goal. See [02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md](02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md).

**Goal** — A sufficiently clarified and confirmed representation of something the Subject wants that can be treated as pursuable/actionable within understood Reality. Distinct from Desire; does not require a Process to already exist. See [02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md](02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md) and [../Goal.md](../Goal.md).

**Reality Check** *(see Clarification)* — A continuous Core capability that surfaces facts, contradictions, assumptions, missing information, risks and opportunities relevant to a Desire or Goal, without removing the Subject's free will. See [02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md](02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md).

**Clarification** — Synonymous with Reality Check in this architecture: the ongoing process of improving clarity about a Desire or Goal against known Reality. See [02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md](02_Desire_Goal_and_Reality_Check.md).

**Decision** — A first-class Core concept: the act of choosing, given a Goal, Process, Reality and authority. Not automatically an Entity; not automatically persisted. See [03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md](03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md).

**Decision Record** — A possible persistent representation of a Decision (context, alternatives, reasoning, authority, expected and actual Outcome). Whether one is created is a Domain/Runtime choice. See [03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md](03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md).

**Decision Memory** — The capability / knowledge structure through which RF-One retains and relates past Decisions, context, reasoning, Outcomes and Learning over time. See [03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md](03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md).

**Action** — Execution, or attempted execution, of a Decision. See [03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md](03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md).

**Outcome** — What actually happens as a result of an Action, as observed in Reality; not assumed identical to what was expected. See [03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md](03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md).

**Learning** — Reusable knowledge generated by comparing expectations, Decision, Action, Reality and Outcome; may update understanding of Reality, the Subject, Processes, assumptions, Decisions, Goals, constraints or models. See [03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md](03_Decision_Action_Outcome_Learning.md).

**Temporal Coherence** — RF-One's ability to evaluate the trajectory produced by accumulated Decisions and Outcomes over time, not just individual Decisions, and to distinguish conscious evolution from drift or inconsistency. See [04_Temporal_Coherence_and_Evolution.md](04_Temporal_Coherence_and_Evolution.md).

**Subject Sovereignty** — The principle that RF-One may challenge, question, and expose consequences, but must not substitute itself for the Subject's final authority over consciously confirmed Desire and strategic direction. See [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md).

**Epistemic Boundary** — The set of distinctions RF-One must maintain between knowledge states (Fact, Observation, Evidence, Belief, Assumption, Inference, Hypothesis, Unknown), so that none is silently converted into another. See [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md).

**Fact** — Something established as true, independent of belief. See [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md).

**Observation** — Something directly perceived or measured. See [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md).

**Evidence** — Information that supports, without necessarily proving, a Fact, Belief or Hypothesis. See [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md).

**Belief** — Something held to be true by the Subject or by RF-One, without being established as Fact. See [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md).

**Assumption** — Something taken as true for the purpose of reasoning, without verification. See [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md).

**Inference** — A conclusion derived from other knowledge, not itself directly observed. See [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md).

**Hypothesis** — A proposed explanation or prediction not yet confirmed. See [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md).

**Unknown** — Something not currently known, explicitly represented as such rather than omitted or silently treated as impossible. See [05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md](05_Epistemic_Boundary_and_Subject_Sovereignty.md).

**Pilot** — The role played by the Subject in the Business Autopilot operating model: defines and confirms strategic direction, Goals, constraints, risk tolerance, authority boundaries and unacceptable Outcomes, and retains override authority. See [06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md](06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md).

**Delegated Authority** — The bounded scope of Decisions and Actions the Pilot has explicitly authorized RF-One to perform without per-instance human approval. May legitimately be "none" for a given Decision class, at the Domain's discretion. See [06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md](06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md).

**Business Autopilot** — RF-One's commercial operating model: RF-One may observe, decide and act continuously within Delegated Authority, escalating to the Pilot when authority, uncertainty, risk or strategic direction require it. Human control does not imply continuous human operation. See [06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md](06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md).

**Intelligence Engine** — An AI model (e.g. an Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or specialized model) that RF-One uses as a reasoning component. A component of RF-One, not RF-One itself; interchangeable. See [06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md](06_Business_Autopilot_and_Intelligence_Engine.md).

**Domain** — A coherent area of business (or other) knowledge that applies and specializes Core concepts. See [../Glossary.md](../Glossary.md) and [00_RF-One_Core_Vision.md](00_RF-One_Core_Vision.md).

**Product** — A commercial application built using one or more Domains, designed to create measurable value for its users. See [00_RF-One_Core_Vision.md](00_RF-One_Core_Vision.md).

**Runtime** — Where actual data, inference, execution, recommendations and actions occur; determines how Core and Domain concepts are instantiated, observed, stored and executed. See [00_RF-One_Core_Vision.md](00_RF-One_Core_Vision.md).

**Gross Outcome** — An Outcome as directly produced by an Action, before any External Obligations / Claims are applied. See [08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md](08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md).

**Net / Retained Outcome** — What the Subject actually retains from a Gross Outcome after relevant External Obligations / Claims. Does not imply every Goal is economic; applies only where the Outcome in question is in fact subject to such claims. See [08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md](08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md).

**External Obligation / Claim** — Something Reality imposes that reduces, redirects, conditions or delays value the Subject would otherwise retain from a Gross Outcome (e.g. taxes, fees, contractual payments, debt service) — illustrative, not a fixed Core taxonomy. See [08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md](08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md).

**Constraint Shaping** — A Subject's lawful Decisions and Actions that change its future relationship to Reality, so that different future constraints, obligations, authority, risks, opportunities or consequences apply. Distinct from violating, concealing or ignoring a Constraint. Does not imply every Constraint is mutable. See [08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md](08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md).

**Counterfactual Structural Comparison** — Comparing a Current Structure against alternative structures using expected Gross/Net Outcome, cost, risk, uncertainty, reversibility, option value, time horizon, flexibility and compliance burden, without collapsing the comparison into a universal scalar score. See [08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md](08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md).

**Lawful Optimization** — Pursuing Constraint Shaping and Counterfactual Structural Comparison within the law, as distinct from evasion, fraud, misrepresentation, concealment, false reporting or sham transactions, which misstate or conceal the Subject's actual relationship to Reality rather than genuinely changing it. See [08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md](08_Net_Outcome_and_Structural_Optimization.md).
